### Traits

_**Thorn Arm.**_ Serava's corrupted demonic arm is lined with hooked barbs. Any creature that grapples Serava or is grappled by her takes 5 (1d10) piercing damage at the start of each of its turns.

_**Tail Sweep.**_ Serava's barbed tail is always moving. She cannot be flanked. Any creature that attempts to move through a space within 5 ft. of her must succeed on a DC 14 Dexterity saving throw or take 7 (2d6) piercing damage and stop moving for that turn.

_**Predator's Mask.**_ The spiked white mask Serava wears unnerves those who face her. Any creature that attempts to read her intentions via Insight automatically fails — her expression is unreadable. She has advantage on Deception checks made through body language or silence.

_**Fluid Aggression.**_ When Serava reduces a creature to 0 HP, she can immediately move up to 15 ft. without provoking opportunity attacks and make one additional Thorn Blade attack.

---

### Actions

_**Multiattack.**_ Serava makes three Thorn Blade attacks. She can replace one attack with a Tail Lash.

_**Thorn Blade.**_ _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 12 (2d6 + 5) slashing damage. On a hit, the target must succeed on a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw or have one of the following applied (Serava's choice):

- **Hooked** — the target's movement speed is reduced by 10 ft. until the start of its next turn.
- **Opened** — the target takes 5 (1d10) piercing damage at the start of its next turn as the wound pulls.
- **Staggered** — the target has disadvantage on its next attack roll.

_**Tail Lash.**_ _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 10 (2d4 + 5) piercing damage and the target is knocked Prone if it fails a DC 15 Strength saving throw.

_**Whirlwind Strike (Recharge 5–6).**_ Serava spins in a blur of blade and thorns. Every creature within 10 ft. must make a DC 16 Dexterity saving throw, taking 28 (8d6) slashing damage on a failed save or half on a success. Each creature that fails is also Hooked — speed reduced by 15 ft. until the end of its next turn.

---

### Bonus Actions

_**Demon Dash.**_ Serava moves up to 20 ft. in a straight line without provoking opportunity attacks. If she moves through a creature's space during this movement, that creature must make a DC 14 Dexterity saving throw or take 7 (2d6) slashing damage from her passing blade.

_**Thorn Grip.**_ Serava attempts to grapple one creature within 5 ft. using her thorned arm (athletics +6 vs target's athletics or acrobatics). A creature grappled this way is also Restrained and takes 5 (1d10) piercing damage at the start of each of its turns until the grapple ends.

---

### Reactions

_**Deflect.**_ When hit by a melee attack, Serava can use her reaction to reduce the damage by 10 (2d4 + 5) as she redirects the blow with her blade.

_**Riposte.**_ When a creature within 5 ft. misses Serava with a melee attack, she can immediately make one Thorn Blade attack against that creature.

---

### Encounter Tactics

Serava picks the most dangerous melee fighter and does not leave them alone. She opens with _Demon Dash_ to close instantly, applies _Thorn Grip_ on the highest-threat target to lock them down, then uses her Multiattack to stack debuffs on anyone nearby. She uses _Whirlwind Strike_ the moment three or more players cluster within 10 ft. — she will deliberately bait this by letting players surround her. _Fluid Aggression_ makes her terrifying against weaker party members; killing a downed player or familiar lets her chain into the next target immediately. She uses _Deflect_ and _Riposte_ to punish any player who commits to a single big attack instead of spreading damage. She never stops moving.

---

### Roleplaying Notes

Serava does not speak during combat. Before it begins she may tilt her masked face slowly toward the player she has already decided to kill first — no words, no threat, just that stillness. A DC 16 Perception check before combat notices the barbed tail is already coiling, the thorned arm already flexing. She has been ready since she walked in.
