### Traits

_**Immovable Sentinel.**_ Gorrath cannot be knocked Prone, pushed, or pulled by any means. Forced movement effects automatically fail against him. He has advantage on all saving throws against spells or effects that would move him.

_**Spiked Bulwark.**_ Any creature that hits Gorrath with a melee attack while he is wielding his tower shield takes 5 (1d10) piercing damage from the spikes as a reaction — no action required.

_**Wall of One.**_ While Gorrath is standing still (has not moved on his last turn), he has a +2 bonus to AC and cannot be flanked.

_**No Face, No Fear.**_ Gorrath has no discernible face beneath his helm. He is immune to any effect that requires eye contact. Spells and abilities that require the caster to see the target's face or read its emotions automatically fail.

_**Demonic Plate.**_ When Gorrath takes 15 or more damage from a single hit, he can use his reaction to reduce that damage by 10 as the corrupted armor absorbs the blow. This can be used 3 times, refreshing on a short or long rest.

---

### Actions

_**Multiattack.**_ Gorrath makes two Broadsword attacks, or one Broadsword attack and one Shield Slam.

_**Broadsword.**_ _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 16 (2d10 + 5) slashing damage. On a critical hit, the target must succeed on a DC 16 Strength saving throw or be knocked Prone.

_**Shield Slam.**_ _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 12 (2d6 + 5) bludgeoning damage and the target must succeed on a DC 16 Strength saving throw or be pushed 10 ft. directly away and have its speed reduced to 0 until the start of its next turn.

_**Warden's Roar (Recharge 5–6).**_ Gorrath slams his sword against his spiked shield with a deafening crash. Each creature within 30 ft. that can hear it must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or be Frightened of Gorrath for 1 minute. A Frightened creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns. Any creature that fails by 5 or more is also Stunned until the end of its next turn.

---

### Bonus Actions

_**Defensive Stance.**_ Gorrath raises his tower shield, gaining half cover (+2 AC, +2 Dex saves) until the start of his next turn. While in Defensive Stance, Shield Slam deals an additional 7 (2d6) damage.

_**Advance.**_ Gorrath moves up to 10 ft. directly toward a creature he can see. This movement does not provoke opportunity attacks.

---

### Reactions

_**Interpose.**_ When an ally within 5 ft. of Gorrath is hit by an attack, Gorrath can use his reaction to become the target of that attack instead. He must be wielding his tower shield for this to work.

_**Punish the Retreating.**_ When a creature within 5 ft. of Gorrath moves away from him, he can make one Broadsword attack against that creature as a reaction, even if he has already used his reaction this round via Interpose. He cannot use both reactions in response to the same trigger.

---

### Encounter Tactics

Gorrath does not chase. He advances steadily, uses _Advance_ as a bonus action to close distance, then planting his feet and forcing players to come to him. He opens with _Warden's Roar_ the moment three or more players are within range. Against spellcasters he prioritizes Shield Slam to push them out of concentration. He uses _Interpose_ to protect any high-value ally in the encounter — if Gorrath is paired with Veyrath or another boss, he will eat hits all day to keep them alive. Players who try to disengage will trigger _Punish the Retreating_ every single time. He fights until destroyed. He does not negotiate. He does not flinch.

---

### Roleplaying Notes

Gorrath does not speak. He has no face. There is nothing to appeal to, no buried humanity to uncover, no leverage to find. A DC 20 Arcana check reveals the armor itself may be the entity — whatever was once inside it is long gone. He exists only to hold the line.
