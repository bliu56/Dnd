### Traits

_**Void Aura.**_ The swirling purple smoke around Veyrath is not decorative — it is alive. Any creature that starts its turn within 5 ft. of Veyrath takes 5 (1d10) necrotic damage as the void eats at them.

_**Arcane Poise.**_ Veyrath does not provoke opportunity attacks when casting spells. He can move up to 15 ft. as a free action immediately after casting a spell (once per turn).

_**The Orb Watches.**_ The glowing arcane orb floating beside Veyrath acts as a ward. Once per round, when Veyrath is hit by an attack, he can halve the damage as a reaction (the orb absorbs it). The orb is destroyed if it absorbs 30 or more damage in a single hit, and this trait cannot be used again until Veyrath uses a bonus action to resummon it.

_**Demonic Focus.**_ Veyrath has advantage on Concentration saving throws. He cannot be disarmed of his sword by any means.

---

### Actions

_**Multiattack.**_ Veyrath makes two Ashen Blade attacks, or one Ashen Blade attack and casts one spell of 2nd level or lower without expending a spell slot.

_**Ashen Blade.**_ _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 13 (2d8 + 4) slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) force damage. On a hit, the target must succeed on a DC 14 Constitution saving throw or have disadvantage on its next attack roll as violet smoke clouds their vision.

_**Arcane Blast.**_ _Ranged Spell Attack:_ +6 to hit, range 60 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 18 (4d8) force damage. If the target is within 10 ft. of another creature, the blast erupts — the second creature must make a DC 14 Dexterity saving throw or take 9 (2d8) force damage.

_**Void Eruption (Recharge 5–6).**_ Veyrath drives his blade into the ground, releasing a surge of arcane void energy. Each creature in a 20 ft. radius must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw, taking 35 (10d6) force damage on a failed save or half on a success. The area becomes difficult terrain filled with purple smoke until the end of Veyrath's next turn.

_**Spellcasting.**_ Veyrath is a 8th-level spellcaster. His spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 14, +6 to hit). He has the following spells prepared:

- Cantrips: _Eldritch Blast, Prestidigitation_
- 1st level (4 slots): _Hex, Shield_
- 2nd level (3 slots): _Misty Step, Darkness_
- 3rd level (2 slots): _Counterspell, Hunger of Hadar_
- 4th level (1 slot): _Banishment_

---

### Bonus Actions

_**Blink Step.**_ Veyrath teleports up to 30 ft. to an unoccupied space he can see, leaving a brief trail of violet smoke. Any creature in the space he arrives in must make a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw or take 7 (2d6) force damage.

_**Resummon the Orb.**_ Veyrath recalls and restores his arcane orb, resetting The Orb Watches trait. The orb resets to 0 absorbed damage.

---

### Reactions

_**Arcane Riposte.**_ When a creature misses Veyrath with a melee attack, he can immediately make one Ashen Blade attack against that creature.

_**Counterspell (3rd level slot).**_ When a creature Veyrath can see within 60 ft. casts a spell, he can use his reaction to attempt to counter it.

---

### Encounter Tactics

Veyrath opens from range with _Hex_ followed by _Arcane Blast_, picking off the squishiest target first. He uses _Blink Step_ aggressively to stay out of melee reach, repositioning after every attack. If two or more players close in on him, he drops _Void Eruption_ and immediately _Misty Steps_ out of the smoke. He saves _Counterspell_ for the party's most powerful caster. The Orb makes him frustrating to burst down — players who focus single-target damage will feel it absorb the big hit. He does not flee until below 20 HP, at which point he uses _Banishment_ as a distraction and vanishes.

---

### Roleplaying Notes

Veyrath does not monologue. He speaks in short, precise sentences and never raises his voice. His demon horn is small — he is not yet fully consumed, and that tension is visible if players look closely. A DC 15 Insight check reveals he is in pain, not rage. Whether that opens a conversation or simply makes him more dangerous is up to them.
