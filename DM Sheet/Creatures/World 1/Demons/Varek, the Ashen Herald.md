### Traits

_**Soul Sense.**_ Varek can sense the life force of every living creature within 60 ft. She knows the exact location of any creature that is not undead or a construct, even through walls and magical darkness. She cannot be surprised.

_**Crown of Thorns.**_ The spiked crown Varek wears is not armor — it is a conduit. Whenever Varek casts a soul spell or uses a soul ability, purple spirit energy crackles along the thorns. Any creature within 10 ft. when this happens must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or be Frightened until the end of its next turn.

_**Death's Threshold.**_ When a creature dies within 60 ft. of Varek, she absorbs a fragment of its departing soul. She regains 10 HP and gains a +1 bonus to attack rolls until the end of her next turn. This cannot trigger more than once per turn.

_**Undying Herald.**_ The first time Varek drops to 0 HP, she instead drops to 1 HP and becomes incorporeal until the start of her next turn — all damage she takes during this time is halved and she cannot be grappled or restrained. After this turn she returns to her physical form fully.

### Actions

_**Multiattack.**_ Varek makes two Soul Cleaver attacks, or one Soul Cleaver attack and uses one soul ability.

_**Soul Cleaver.**_ _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 17 (2d12 + 4) slashing damage plus 9 (2d8) necrotic damage. On a hit the target must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or have its HP maximum reduced by the necrotic damage dealt until it finishes a long rest. A creature whose HP maximum is reduced to 0 this way dies and cannot be resurrected by any spell of 5th level or lower.

_**Soul Rend.**_ _Ranged Spell Attack:_ +7 to hit, range 60 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 18 (4d8) necrotic damage and the target must succeed on a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or be Stunned until the end of its next turn as Varek tears at its soul directly. On a successful save the target is instead Incapacitated until the end of its next turn.

_**Reaping Wave (Recharge 5–6).**_ Varek sweeps her Soul Cleaver in a wide arc while releasing a burst of spirit energy. Each creature in a 15 ft. cone must make a DC 16 Dexterity saving throw, taking 21 (6d6) slashing damage and 14 (4d6) necrotic damage on a failed save, or half on a success. Any creature that fails this save also has its speed halved until the end of its next turn as ghostly chains drag at its limbs.

_**Soul Grasp.**_ Varek reaches out with the grey wisp of soul energy in her off-hand toward one creature within 30 ft. The target must make a DC 15 Charisma saving throw. On a failure Varek takes control of the target's soul briefly — the target cannot use reactions and must use its next action to move toward Varek. On a success the target takes 14 (4d6) psychic damage and the effect ends.

---

### Bonus Actions

_**Herald's Step.**_ Varek vanishes in a curl of purple smoke and reappears in an unoccupied space within 30 ft. Any creature within 5 ft. of her departure point must make a DC 14 Wisdom saving throw or take 7 (2d6) necrotic damage as residual soul energy lashes out.

_**Mark for Reaping.**_ Varek marks one creature she can see within 60 ft. Until the end of the encounter or until the creature dies, Varek's attacks against that creature deal an additional 7 (2d6) necrotic damage and the creature cannot regain HP from any source other than magical healing of 3rd level or higher.

---

### Reactions

_**Soul Anchor.**_ When a creature within 30 ft. attempts to teleport or use any form of planar travel, Varek can use her reaction to attempt to prevent it. The creature must succeed on a DC 15 Charisma saving throw or the movement fails and the creature takes 14 (4d6) necrotic damage.

_**Spectral Deflection.**_ When Varek is hit by an attack, she can use her reaction to briefly become partially incorporeal — reducing the damage by 14 (4d6). If this reduces the damage to 0, the attack passes through her entirely and she is unaffected.

---

### Encounter Tactics

Varek opens by using _Mark for Reaping_ on whoever looks most dangerous, then closes immediately to melee range. She uses _Soul Grasp_ on any player attempting to flee or stay at range, dragging them back into the axe. She saves _Reaping Wave_ for when three or more players cluster — she will deliberately walk into groups to bait this. _Death's Threshold_ makes killing her allies in the encounter actively dangerous for the party, as each death makes her stronger. Players who try to disengage via teleportation will find _Soul Anchor_ waiting for them. When _Undying Herald_ triggers she uses _Herald's Step_ immediately to reposition and opens her next turn with _Soul Rend_ on whoever tried to finish her.

---

### Roleplaying Notes

Varek does not speak to the living as equals. If she addresses the players at all it is in the tone of a bureaucrat cataloguing names — detached, certain, final. She has come to collect and she has no concept of failure. A DC 17 Religion check reveals that death heralds are not evil by nature — they serve a function. Whatever sent Varek here specifically, to this place, for these people, is the real question worth asking.
