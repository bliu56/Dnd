### Traits

_**Blood Sense.**_ Caelen can smell blood within 60 ft. He always knows the current HP total of any creature that has taken damage this encounter. He has advantage on attack rolls against any creature below half its HP maximum.

_**Hemorrhagic Aura.**_ Any creature that starts its turn within 10 ft. of Caelen while Bleeding (see below) takes an additional 3 (1d6) necrotic damage as Caelen passively draws the blood outward.

_**Demonic Resilience.**_ When Caelen drops to 0 HP for the first time in an encounter, he instead drops to 1 HP and his demon manifests briefly — all creatures within 15 ft. must make a DC 14 Wisdom saving throw or be Frightened until the end of their next turn. This trait cannot be used again until a long rest.

_**Sigil Cloak.**_ The arcane sigils on Caelen's cloak grant him advantage on saving throws against spells that force movement or restrain him.

---

### Conditions

_**Bleeding.**_ A creature that is Bleeding takes 5 (1d10) necrotic damage at the start of each of its turns. A creature can end this condition by using its action to make a DC 13 Medicine check, or by receiving any magical healing.

---

### Actions

_**Multiattack.**_ Caelen makes two Blood Lance attacks, or one Blood Lance and casts one blood spell.

_**Blood Lance.**_ _Ranged Spell Attack:_ +7 to hit, range 60 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 14 (3d6 + 4) necrotic damage and the target must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or begin Bleeding.

_**Crimson Burst.**_ _Area Attack:_ Caelen detonates the blood inside a Bleeding creature within 60 ft. The target takes 21 (6d6) necrotic damage (no save — the blood is already inside them) and the Bleeding condition ends. Each creature within 10 ft. of the target must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw or take 10 (3d6) necrotic damage from the spray.

_**Sanguine Chains (Recharge 5–6).**_ Caelen raises his hand and blood erupts from the ground in a 20 ft. radius centered on a point he can see within 60 ft. Each creature in the area must make a DC 15 Strength saving throw or be Restrained as hardened blood coils around them. A Restrained creature can repeat the save at the end of each of its turns. Any creature that is already Bleeding has disadvantage on this saving throw.

_**Drain.**_ _Melee Spell Attack:_ +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 18 (4d8) necrotic damage and Caelen regains HP equal to half the damage dealt. The target begins Bleeding if it isn't already.

---

### Bonus Actions

_**Open the Wound.**_ Caelen chooses one Bleeding creature within 60 ft. That creature's Bleeding damage increases from 1d10 to 2d10 until the condition is removed. Can only be applied once per creature per encounter.

_**Crimson Step.**_ Caelen teleports up to 20 ft. to an unoccupied space, leaving a splatter of blood behind. Any creature that moves through that space before the start of Caelen's next turn must succeed on a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw or begin Bleeding.

---

### Reactions

_**Blood Shield.**_ When Caelen is hit by an attack, he can use his reaction to spend the blood of a Bleeding creature within 30 ft. — that creature takes 9 (2d8) necrotic damage and Caelen reduces the incoming damage by the same amount.

_**Redirect.**_ When targeted by a spell of 3rd level or lower, Caelen can use his reaction to make a DC 13 + spell level Arcana check. On a success, he redirects the spell to a Bleeding creature within 60 ft. as the blood in their body draws the magic toward them.

---

### Encounter Tactics

Caelen's entire gameplan is to get one or two players Bleeding as fast as possible, then use _Open the Wound_ and _Crimson Burst_ as a kill combo. He opens with Blood Lance on the lowest-CON player, follows with _Open the Wound_, then spends subsequent turns applying Bleeding to a second target while letting the first deteriorate. He uses _Blood Shield_ whenever he takes a big hit — ideally burning a Bleeding ally to tank it. _Sanguine Chains_ is his panic button when players close in. He never stands still, using _Crimson Step_ every round if possible to keep distance and leave hazardous terrain behind him. Players who ignore the Bleeding condition and push damage will find him harder and harder to kill as _Drain_ keeps him topped up.

---

### Roleplaying Notes

Caelen is not monstrous in appearance — he looks young, almost uncomfortable, the demon horn small and half-hidden in his hair. He speaks calmly and with detachment, as if discussing the weather while blood crawls across the floor toward him. A DC 14 Insight check reveals genuine revulsion beneath the control — he has not made peace with what he is. Whether players can exploit that before he kills them is the question.
