### Traits

_**Skull-Bound.**_ The skulls hanging from The Chained One's body are trophies of those it has slain. It has advantage on saving throws against being banished. For every creature it has killed in the current encounter, it gains a cumulative +1 bonus to damage rolls (max +5).

_**Inexorable.**_ The Chained One cannot be knocked Prone, pushed, or pulled by any effect of CR 8 or lower. Teleportation and forced movement effects automatically fail against it.

_**Demonic Resilience.**_ If The Chained One starts its turn with 68 HP or fewer, it regains 10 HP at the start of each of its turns. This trait does not function if The Chained One has taken radiant damage since its last turn.

_**Crushing Presence.**_ Any creature that starts its turn within 10 ft. of The Chained One must succeed on a DC 15 Strength saving throw or have its speed reduced to 0 until the start of its next turn.

---

### Actions

_**Multiattack.**_ The Chained One makes two attacks: one with its Spiked Maul and one with its Chain Lash. It cannot make both Chain Lash attacks and a Maul attack in the same turn.

_**Spiked Maul.**_ _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 22 (3d10 + 6) bludgeoning + piercing damage. If this attack reduces a creature to 0 HP, all creatures within 15 ft. must succeed on a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw or be Frightened until the end of their next turn.

_**Chain Lash.**_ _Melee Weapon Attack:_ +9 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. _Hit:_ 14 (2d6 + 6) slashing damage and the target is Grappled (escape DC 17). While grappled, the target is Restrained. The Chained One can have up to two creatures grappled at once via this attack.

_**Drag Down (Recharge 5–6).**_ Each creature currently Grappled by The Chained One is slammed into the ground. Each must make a DC 17 Strength saving throw, taking 28 (8d6) bludgeoning damage and falling Prone on a failed save, or half damage and no Prone on a success. The grapple then ends.

---

### Bonus Actions

_**Rattle the Chains.**_ The Chained One shakes its chains violently. Each creature within 30 ft. that can hear it must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw or be Frightened until the end of its next turn.

---

### Reactions

_**Dead Weight.**_ When a creature within 15 ft. attempts to move away from The Chained One, it can make one Chain Lash attack against that creature as a reaction. On a hit, the creature's movement is reduced to 0 for that turn.

---

### Encounter Tactics

The Chained One moves toward the largest cluster of players and does not stop. It prioritizes Chain Lash to Restrain the most mobile threat, then uses its Spiked Maul on whoever is closest. It never retreats — it simply keeps walking forward, chains dragging, head bowed, until something stops it. Use _Drag Down_ the moment two creatures are grappled. If players attempt to flee, _Dead Weight_ punishes them for it.

---

### Roleplaying Notes

The Chained One does not speak. It does not react to taunts or pleas. It simply walks forward, chains dragging, skulls clattering, and does not stop until everything in front of it is dead. There is no person left inside — only purpose. A DC 18 Religion check suggests this was once a paladin or holy warrior, bound to its own sins long after the man inside died.
