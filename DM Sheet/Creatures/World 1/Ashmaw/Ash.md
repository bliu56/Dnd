CR 7 _Medium Humanoid (Demon), Neutral Evil_

_Tall, lean, and still. He moves with the economy of something that has never needed to waste motion. His eyes are gold and slightly too still for a human face. The missing left eye carried over, present but filmed over, pale and sightless. People who spend time near him find themselves uneasy without being able to say why._

**AC:** 16 (natural resilience) | **HP:** 130 (17d8 + 51) | **Speed:** 50 ft., climb 40 ft.

|STR|DEX|CON|INT|WIS|CHA|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|19 (+4)|22 (+6)|16 (+3)|14 (+2)|16 (+3)|10 (+0)|

**Saving Throws:** Dex +9, Wis +6 **Skills:** Perception +6, Stealth +12, Survival +6, Insight +6 **Damage Resistances:** Fire, Necrotic **Senses:** Darkvision 120 ft., Passive Perception 16 **Languages:** Common, Abyssal

**Traits**

**_Blind Side._** Ash has a blind spot on his left. Attacks made from directly to his left have advantage. He has disadvantage on Perception checks involving his left side.

**_Trap Sense._** Ash has advantage on saving throws and ability checks to detect or avoid traps. He will not approach a location that feels deliberately prepared.

**_Pounce._** If Ash moves at least 20 ft. toward a target and hits with a Blade Strike, the target must make a DC 15 Strength save or be knocked prone. If prone, Ash can make a second Blade Strike as a bonus action.

**_Patient Hunter._** If Ash does not attack on his turn, he has advantage on his first attack roll on his next turn.

**_Corrupted Instinct._** Ash cannot be surprised while conscious and has advantage on initiative rolls.

**Actions**

**_Multiattack._** Ash makes two Blade Strike attacks.

**_Blade Strike._** +9 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 3d8+6 slashing.

**_Calculated Burst._** Ash makes three Blade Strike attacks in rapid succession against one target. Each hit after the first deals an additional 1d6 damage. Recharge 5-6.

**_Vanish._** Ash takes the Hide action and moves up to half his speed without provoking opportunity attacks. He cannot be tracked by scent or sound until the start of his next turn.

**Bonus Actions**

**_Read the Opening._** Ash studies one creature within 60 ft. Until the end of his next turn he has advantage on all attack rolls against that creature and ignores half and three-quarters cover.

**Will — Stage I: Speed**

**_Blur._** As a bonus action Ash pushes his Will into his body. Until the end of his next turn his movement speed doubles, he can move through other creatures without provoking opportunity attacks, and the first Blade Strike he makes this turn deals an additional 2d6 damage as his speed becomes force. Once per short rest.

**_Afterimage._** When Ash uses Blur, creatures that attempt to attack him before the start of his next turn must make a DC 15 Wisdom save or attack the afterimage instead, automatically missing. Creatures that succeed see through it.

**Will — Stage II: Precision**

**_Precision Strike._** Once per turn when Ash hits with a Blade Strike while he has advantage on the attack roll, the attack deals an additional 3d6 damage and the target cannot use reactions until the start of their next turn. The advantage represents him reading the opening clearly enough to exploit it.

**_Calculated Aggression._** Once per long rest Ash combines both stages of his Will. He activates Blur and immediately makes a full Multiattack against one target. Each attack is made with advantage. Any hit made with advantage automatically triggers Precision Strike. After the attacks resolve the target must make a DC 16 Constitution save or be stunned until the end of their next turn. Once per long rest.

**Reactions**

**_Sidestep._** When Ash is targeted by a ranged attack, he moves up to 10 ft. in any direction. If this movement takes him out of the attack's path, the attack misses automatically.

**_Precision Counter._** When a creature misses Ash with a melee attack, he can make one Blade Strike against them as a reaction. If this hits and Ash has advantage on the roll, it automatically triggers Precision Strike.
