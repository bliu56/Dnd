**Jin — The Timid Snake**

*Sectless — Wandering Traveler*
*No martial level (Traveler)*

---

## Who He Is

| | |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Jin |
| **Sect** | None — claims to be a wandering nobody |
| **True nature** | A "snake." Secretly a Traveler, like the party. Waiting for an opportunity to take them out and steal everything they have. |
| **Abilities** | Built for betrayal: **pocket hoarding** (stash things in his pockets), **ambush** (strike when least expected) |
| **Possessions** | A sword and a rusted bracelet — the bracelet is his trophy from his first mark |

**The act:** Weak, timid, afraid. Alone and desperate. He'll cling to the party and do anything — even become a slave — to stay with them.

---

## The Encounter

He approaches alone, trembling. Claims to be lost, frightened, useless with a blade. Begs to stay close to the party — just for safety, just until he finds his companions.

He plays the pitiful role perfectly. The party's guard drops. That's when he waits.

**The trap:** The moment a good opportunity presents itself — alone, distracted, vulnerable — he strikes.

---

## Lines

| Moment | Lines |
|---|---|
| **First approach** | *(trembling)* *"P-please... please, I'm lost. I don't know where my group went. Can I— can I just stay near you? I won't be any trouble, I swear."* |
| **Begging** | *"I'll do anything. Anything at all. Carry your things, watch your backs, take the first hit — I just don't want to die alone out here."* |
| **Offering himself** | *"If you want, I could be your servant. Your slave. I don't have pride — I have survival. That's all I've ever had."* |
| **Playing weak** | *(flinching at shadows)* *"D-did you hear that? There's something out there. There's always something out there."* |
| **Feigning loyalty** | *"You're the first people who've been kind to me in years. I'd die for you. I really would."* |
| **The moment before betrayal** | *(quiet, almost to himself)* *"...You really trusted me, didn't you?"* |
| **When caught** | *(no more trembling — flat, cold)* *"Ah. You're sharper than you look. Shame. I liked you."* |
| **If he wins** | *(pocketing their things, whistling)* *"Generous to the end. I'll remember that."* |

---

## DM Notes

- His betrayal should feel earned — the longer he stays, the more the party trusts him, the harder it lands
- Good timing for the strike: when the party is split, wounded, or distracted by a bigger threat
- If caught early, he's genuinely fast — but he'll surrender quickly if cornered. A snake bites once, then flees
- The rusted bracelet: if asked, he says it's a family heirloom. It's from his first mark — a promise to himself that everyone can be fooled
