**Lin Shuang (林霜) — The Young Adult Girl**

*White Crane Clan — First Tier (One of the Three Peaks)*
*Low-mid Martial Master*

---

## Who She Is

| | |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Lin Shuang — "frost" |
| **Sect** | White Crane Clan, first-tier. One of the **Three Peaks** |
| **Level** | Low-mid Martial Master — precise, methodical |
| **Master** | Clan head Lin Tian (Transcendent) — sworn brother of Old Xu |
| **Nature** | Cold, very methodical, righteous |

**Her role:** She is the person **Old Xu** is trying to keep safe — the daughter of her clan head's sworn brother, protected from the shadows.

---

## The Encounter

The party meets her — cold, composed, every motion deliberate. She is a disciple of a great clan and carries herself with that weight.

She may be encountered before or after the Drunk Old Man. If they've met him, the pieces connect when they see them together — or she mentions the old drunk who's been following her.

She is good. Righteous. But her righteousness is ice — no warmth, no compromise.

---

## Lines

| Moment | Lines |
|---|---|
| **Greeting** | *(level gaze)* *"You are not of any sect I recognize. State your business."* |
| **Assessing them** | *"You carry yourselves well. But strength without discipline is just violence waiting to happen."* |
| **On rules** | *"There are rules in this realm. Follow them, and you will find no trouble from me. Break them, and I will correct you."* |
| **If they help someone** | *(a slight thaw)* *"...That was well done. Not everyone remembers mercy in a place like this."* |
| **On her clan** | *"White Crane does not kneel. We do not beg. We endure, and we strike true."* |
| **On Old Xu** | *(annoyed, fond)* *"There is an old drunk following me. He thinks I haven't noticed. He is... a friend of my father's. Do not mock him — I will not allow it."* |
| **If threatened** | *"You misunderstand what I am. I am not prey. I am the crane."* |
| **Righteous fury** | *"You have wronged the innocent. I am the judgment you will not escape."* |

---

## DM Notes

- Cold but good — the party can earn her respect through righteous actions, not flattery
- She is aware of Old Xu's presence (or at least suspects it) and is protective of him despite her annoyance
- Her father, Lin Tian, is a Transcendent — hurting her has massive consequences
- She could become a strong ally — or a cold enemy — depending on how the party treats innocents in the realm
