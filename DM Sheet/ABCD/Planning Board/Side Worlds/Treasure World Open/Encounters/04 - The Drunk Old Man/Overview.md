**Old Xu (徐老头) — The Drunk Old Man**

*Sectless — Retired*
*Martial Grandmaster at best (hides it)*

---

## Who He Is

| | |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Old Xu — "Xu the old man" |
| **Sect** | None. Retired. |
| **Level** | Martial Grandmaster at best — hides it behind laziness and drink |
| **Nature** | Unstressed. Free. Dislikes being chained down. Acts lazy and uncaring, but has a good heart |
| **Why he's here** | A friend asked him to take care of someone. He got separated from them. |

**His past:** His disciple died. He vowed to never take another. That's why he deflects with laziness and indifference — closeness means loss.

---

## The Encounter

The party has a challenge to complete, but the drunk old man keeps asking them for food. He's completely unstressed — unlike everyone else in the realm. Nothing fazes him.

He's very strong. He just doesn't want to be bothered.

---

## Lines

| Moment | Lines |
|---|---|
| **First words** | *(lounging, waving a bottle)* *"Hey. You. Got anything to eat? I'm starving and this realm doesn't have a tavern."* |
| **Asked if he's not worried** | *"Worried? About what? I'm old, drunk, and retired. The realm can't hurt me — I've seen worse hangovers."* |
| **On the challenge** | *"Oh, you want me to take this seriously? Fine, fine. But it goes better on a full stomach. Got any food?"* |
| **On his strength** | *(yawning)* *"I used to be someone. Now I'm just someone who wants a nap and a meal."* |
| **On his disciple** | *(the only time his eyes sharpen)* *"...I had one, once. Never again. Don't ask."* |
| **If pressed about why he's here** | *"A friend asked me to watch over someone. Good kid, that one. Sworn brother's daughter. But I lost her in this mess — so now I'm just eating and waiting for her to show up."* |
| **If threatened** | *(a slow, dangerous smile)* *"Oh? You sure about that? I'm too old to fight properly — I might hurt myself. Or you."* |
| **After a fight** | *(dusting off, unbothered)* *"See? That's why I don't bother. Too much work. Now — food?"* |

---

## DM Notes

- He is the **young girl's hidden protector** (see Encounter 05) — sworn brother's daughter
- Never takes a disciple. The party might try to earn his mentorship — it will not work, and they should feel that wall
- When he fights, he fights like a Grandmaster — but only when pushed. His default is dodging, yawning, and disarming gently
- Good source of exposition if the party earns his goodwill — he knows the realm's history better than anyone
