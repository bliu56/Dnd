**Jin Hao — The Predator**

*Verdant Serpent Hall (碧蛇堂) — Second Tier*
*High Martial Disciple*

"He smiles like he's granting a favor. He's not. He's taking inventory."

---

**Role:** Clan head of the Verdant Serpent Hall
**Level:** High Martial Disciple (CR 6)
**Sect:** Verdant Serpent Hall — second tier
**Escort:** Mo Yan (right hand) + 2–3 Serpent Retainers

---

## Who He Is

- Young clan head who inherited early. Used to getting what he wants
- Wants **Xiao Yun** as a **concubine** — and sees her desperation as leverage, not tragedy
- Charming on the surface. Offers false promises: patronage, funds, food for her starving sect, a place for her brother to train properly
- The price is never said outright — but it's always there. She knows. He knows she knows
- Patient. He can afford to be. He thinks of it as courtship; everyone else sees a trap
- He's done nothing *illegal* in the realm's eyes — that's his armor. Offending him openly means offending a second-tier sect for no "real" reason

**Fighting style:** Elegant, serpentine. Swords + poisons + hidden weapons. He doesn't fight fair — but he makes it look beautiful.

## DM Notes

- He's a social predator first, a fighter second. The party should *want* to hurt him long before they can justify it
- His power is his status. Removing that — exposing his intentions, outmaneuvering him politically — hurts him more than any wound
- If cornered in combat, he fights to escape, not to win. He'll poison, dart, and vanish — and remember
- **Mo Yan** is his shadow and his enabler. She does the dirty work so his hands stay clean

---

## Story Notes (DM Only)

- He can return as a recurring antagonist — a second-tier clan head with a grudge is dangerous
- If the party humiliates him, he may target Falling Leaf out of spite — the annihilation could be *his* doing instead of the original plan
- If Xiao Yun refuses him and the party backs her, it's a statement: Falling Leaf has protectors. Word spreads
