**Mo Yan — The Ink Swallow**

*Verdant Serpent Hall (碧蛇堂) — Second Tier*
*Mid Martial Disciple*

"She never speaks unless spoken to. She never misses. She is the shadow the serpent casts."

---

**Role:** Jin Hao's right hand — escort, enforcer, and the one who does the dirty work
**Level:** Mid Martial Disciple (CR 4)
**Sect:** Verdant Serpent Hall — second tier
**Master:** Serves Jin Hao, clan head

---

## Who She Is

- Quiet, precise, and utterly loyal to Jin Hao
- Fights with hidden weapons and poison — needles, darts, a weighted chain
- She enables Jin Hao's schemes: handling threats, escorting "guests," making problems disappear
- Her hands are dirty so his stay clean. She accepts this without comment
- Whether she believes in him or simply has nowhere else to go is unclear — she never says

---

## DM Notes

- She's the quiet threat in the room — more dangerous than she looks, entirely expendable to Jin Hao's plans
- Her loyalty is her weakness: protecting Jin Hao makes her predictable
- If the party can reach her — turn her, or at least make her hesitate — Jin Hao loses his shadow
- She fights to disable and escape, not to kill, unless ordered otherwise

---

## Story Notes (DM Only)

- Possible redemption or betrayal hook: if Jin Hao is defeated or humiliated, she may break from him — or double down
- She knows where all his secrets are buried. Literally
