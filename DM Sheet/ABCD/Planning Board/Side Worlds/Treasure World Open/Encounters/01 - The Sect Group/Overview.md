**Xiao Yun & Xiao Feng — The Sect Group**

*Falling Leaf Sect (落叶门)*
*Third Tier — trying to rise*

---

## Who They Are

| | |
|---|---|
| **Xiao Yun** (girl, young adult) | Leader. Mid-high Martial Disciple. Polite, careful, calculating. Treats the party as seniors — they must be strong. Can't afford to offend them. |
| **Xiao Feng** (boy, ~15) | Her younger brother. Lower Martial Disciple. Eager, less guarded than his sister. |
| **Sect members** | ~10, all around their age or younger. Low Martial Disciple. |
| **Master Chen Weiming** | Martial Master at least (was stronger before the attack). Elderly, injured. Sent the siblings in his place. |

**The sect:** Was once higher, fell after an attack long ago. Poor — they haven't eaten meat in a long time. They're here to gain power and money to lift the sect back up.

---

## The Encounter

The party meets them in the field. The girl immediately treats the party as **seniors** — no known sect uniform, so they must be sectless, but they look *strong*. Their luxurious clothes suggest high standing.

- She announces and identifies herself and her sect
- Asks what the party's goal is — carefully, not wanting to say the wrong thing or provoke them

In the field nearby: a treasure chest guarded by a few monsters *(monsters TBD)*. The party decides what to do — fight, avoid, ignore.

**Branch — Favorable impression:**
- Xiao Feng asks if the party would please travel with them
- Xiao Yun rebukes him — they can't trouble or burden such seniors; they're too weak to be worth their time
- Players decide the outcome

**Branch — Neutral / slightly favorable:**
- Xiao Yun asks if she may continue to refer to them as "Senior"
- Asks their names
- Wishes them good luck
- Hopes she can repay them in the future

## DM Notes

- The party's help shapes their fate — see the Plot Point below
- **Tangible gifts** → they become targets (ants carrying treasure). More misfortune.
- **Intangible gifts** (training, knowledge, protection) → they grow, and survive longer
- At every new location, the party may run into them again

---

## Plot Point — The Sect's Fate

**After separation:** The party finds them later — slaughtered.

**Outcome options:** only the girl survives / only the boy survives / both survive / both + some members survive / none survive

**How the annihilation happens (girl's route):**
- They tried an **orange gate** — and got lucky at first
- The next gate was bad luck: they ran into people **stronger than them** who seemed like good people
- They were not good people. They attacked directly, with no warning — overwhelming strength.
- By the time the party finds them, the group has been slaughtered — only the outcome survivors remain
