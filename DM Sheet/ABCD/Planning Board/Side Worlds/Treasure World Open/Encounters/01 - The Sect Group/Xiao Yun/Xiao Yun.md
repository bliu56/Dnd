*Falling Leaf Sect (落叶门) — Third Tier*
*Mid Martial Disciple*

"She corrects your stance before you finish drawing your weapon. She isn't trying to embarrass you. She genuinely believes good form is a kindness."

---

**Role:** Leader of the Falling Leaf Sect delegation
**Level:** Mid Martial Disciple (CR 4)
**Sect:** Falling Leaf Sect — third tier, trying to rise
**Master:** [[../../../../../World 1/Characters/None|Chen Weiming]] (placeholder — Martial Master at least)
**Companions:** Brother Xiao Feng + ~10 sect members

---

## Who She Is

- Young adult. Carries herself with the weight of leading a sect that's fallen far and wants to rise
- Polite, careful, calculating. Treats the party as seniors — no known sect uniform, but they look strong. Their clothes suggest high standing. She can't afford to offend them
- Poor sect — they haven't eaten meat in a long time. She's here for power and money to lift Falling Leaf back up
- Genuinely kind in her own disciplined way — she corrects form because she believes good form is a kindness, not an insult

## Catchphrases

| Moment | Lines |
|---|---|
| **Greeting** | *"This one greets the seniors. I am Xiao Yun of the Falling Leaf Sect. We are honored to cross paths with such distinguished travelers."* |
| **Identifying themselves** | *"We are but a humble third-tier sect. We mean no offense — we merely seek fortune in this realm, as all do."* |
| **Asking their goal** | *"May this one ask what the seniors seek within the realm? I ask only so that we may avoid treading where we are not welcome."* |
| **Xiao Feng's plea** | *"Feng. Enough. The seniors have their own path. We are too weak to burden them — it would be disrespectful to ask."* |
| **Neutral farewell** | *"Then this one bids the seniors good fortune. If ever Falling Leaf can repay your kindness, we will remember."* |
| **On their poverty** | *"We are not ashamed of our hunger. We are ashamed of what we have failed to become."* |
| **When offered tangible treasure** | *(reluctant, eyes hungry)* *"Seniors are too generous... we do not deserve such gifts."* *(she takes them anyway — and they will draw eyes)* |
| **When offered training/guidance** | *(surprised, then deeply grateful)* *"This one... this one does not know how to thank the seniors. We will not forget this."* |

---

## DM Notes

- **Tangible gifts** → they become targets (ants carrying treasure). More misfortune.
- **Intangible gifts** (training, knowledge, protection) → they grow, and survive longer
- At every new location, the party may run into them again
- See the **Sect's Fate** plot point in the encounter overview

---

## Story Notes (DM Only)

- The party's choices shape whether she lives or dies when the annihilation happens
- If she survives alone, she may return hardened — a future rival or ally
- Her growth: if gifted intangible aid, she can rise toward Martial Master (CR 7–8) over time
