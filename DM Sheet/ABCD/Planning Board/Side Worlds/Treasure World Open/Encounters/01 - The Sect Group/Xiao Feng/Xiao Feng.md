**Xiao Feng**

*Falling Leaf Sect (落叶门) — Third Tier*
*Lower Martial Disciple — Future Clan Head*

"Eager, loud, and terrified of failing his sister. He swings first and thinks second — but he is trying so hard to be the person they need him to be."

---

**Role:** Future clan head of Falling Leaf Sect (heir)
**Level:** Lower Martial Disciple (CR 2)
**Sect:** Falling Leaf Sect — third tier, trying to rise
**Master:** His grandparent, Chen Weiming
**Sister:** Xiao Yun (group leader, holding his place until he's ready)

---

## Who He Is

- ~15 years old. The heir of Falling Leaf — the sect's future rests on his shoulders, even though he's still a boy
- Eager, loud, less guarded than his sister. He asks the question she wouldn't dare to
- Knows his sister is holding the lead for him. It weighs on him — he wants to be worthy of it
- His parents died in the attack years ago, same as hers. He was too young to remember them clearly
- He's not ready to lead. He knows it. Everyone knows it. That's why he's here — to grow into it

## DM Notes

- He asks Xiao Yun the question she can't — he's the unguarded heart of the group
- If he's in danger, Xiao Yun's composure cracks. Protecting him is her first instinct
- His growth: if the party helps him (intangible gifts), he could advance toward mid Disciple — and his readiness to lead matters for the sect's fate

---

## Story Notes (DM Only)

- His survival in the annihilation matters twice: to the sect's future, and to Xiao Yun's story
- If he dies and she lives, she may be consumed by guilt — she was supposed to protect the heir
- If he lives and she dies, he inherits the weight early — a different, harder boy
- The party's intangible gifts shape which future happens
