**Realm Structure & Lore**

---

## The Realm

An open realm that appears unpredictably — never the same location, though occasionally it *does* repeat. No known owner. No known origin. It just appears, stays open, then vanishes.

Inside: creatures, monsters, treasure, opportunities. Sects send disciples in when it opens. Travelers compete alongside them.

---

## The 4 Locations

Color-coded by difficulty:

| Color | Difficulty |
|---|---|
| **Gray** | Beginner |
| **Orange** | Intermediate |
| **Red** | Expert |
| **Black** | Anything goes |

---

## How Entry Works

- Everyone is placed into a random set location (e.g., a beachfront) — a starting zone
- Can't travel past it — there's an **invisible world border**
- After clearing the room's challenge, **magic gates** pop open
- Gate color determines difficulty and reward
- **White Gate** — takes people *out* if they choose to leave
- Once a challenge starts, you **can't leave until completion**

## Progression Rule

- In a gray area → mostly gray gates, but **always one orange gate**
- Through the orange gate → mostly orange, one red
- You can go **up** in level, **never down**

---

## Sect Tiers

- **First Tier** — the three dominant sects. Collectively known as **"The Three Peaks"** (三峰). Not allied — just the three strongest, universally acknowledged.
- **Second Tier** — strong, established, but below the Three Peaks
- **Third Tier** — struggling, trying to rise
- **Sectless / Wandering** — no sect backing (Travelers, lone wolves)

**Current Three Peaks:** Shaolin Temple, White Crane Clan, + one open slot (TBD)

## Martial Levels

Personal strength ladder (with in-between grades — low / mid / high):

**Martial Disciple** → **Martial Master** → **Martial Grandmaster** → **Transcendent**

- Only the very top reaches Transcendent
- Travelers do not use this system — they have no martial level
