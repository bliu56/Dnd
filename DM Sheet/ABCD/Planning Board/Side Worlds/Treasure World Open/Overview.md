**Treasure World — Overview**

*Type: Side World / Competition Arc*

**Concept:** A recurring realm that opens periodically — a treasure hunt where Travelers compete to hunt the biggest and strongest creatures. Think wuxia treasure realm: a space full of rare monsters, valuable materials, and glory. Whoever participates in a given session gets the rewards; those who don't, miss out.

**The Competition:**
- Travelers from across worlds are drawn to the realm when it opens
- Goal: hunt and claim the most valuable quarry
- Rankings are tracked — the realm itself seems to remember who's won before
- Alliances form and break. Backstabbing is expected. Friends and enemies are made here.

**Why it works for the group:**
- Player-count flexible — whoever shows up enters the hunt; absent players simply didn't participate that opening
- Every session is self-contained (a hunt) while the meta-progression (rankings, reputation, relationships with other Travelers) persists
- Rewards are tangible: rare materials, monster parts, treasure

**Tone:** Rivalry. Glory. Greed. Risk.
