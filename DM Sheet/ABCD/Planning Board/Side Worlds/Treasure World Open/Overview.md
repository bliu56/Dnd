**Treasure World — Overview**

*Type: Side World / Competition Arc*

**Concept:** A recurring realm that opens periodically — a treasure hunt where Travelers compete to hunt the biggest and strongest creatures. Think wuxia treasure realm: a space full of rare monsters, valuable materials, and glory. Whoever participates in a given session gets the rewards; those who don't, miss out.

**The Competition:**
- Travelers from across worlds are drawn to the realm when it opens
- Goal: hunt and claim the most valuable quarry
- Rankings are tracked — the realm itself seems to remember who's won before
- Alliances form and break. Backstabbing is expected. Friends and enemies are made here.

**Why it works for the group:**
- Player-count flexible — whoever shows up enters the hunt; absent players simply didn't participate that opening
- Every session is self-contained (a hunt) while the meta-progression (rankings, reputation, relationships with other Travelers) persists
- Rewards are tangible: rare materials, monster parts, treasure

**Tone:** Rivalry. Glory. Greed. Risk.

## The Realm Structure

**4 locations, color-coded by difficulty:**
- **Gray** — beginner
- **Orange** — intermediate
- **Red** — expert
- **Black** — anything goes

**How entry works:**
- Everyone is placed into a random set location (e.g., a beachfront) — a starting zone
- Can't travel past it — there's an **invisible world border**
- After clearing the room's challenge, **magic gates** pop open
- Gate color determines difficulty and reward
- **White Gate** — takes people *out* if they choose to leave
- Once a challenge starts, you **can't leave until completion**

**Progression rule:**
- In a gray area → mostly gray gates, but **always one orange gate**
- Through the orange gate → mostly orange, one red
- You can go **up** in level, **never down**

**Note:** The realm appears unpredictably — never the same location, though occasionally it *does* repeat. No known owner. No known origin. It just appears, stays open, then vanishes.

## Sect Tiers

- **First Tier** — the three dominant sects. Collectively known as **"The Three Peaks"** (三峰). Not allied — just the three strongest, universally acknowledged.
- **Second Tier** — strong, established, but below the Three Peaks
- **Third Tier** — struggling, trying to rise
- **Sectless / Wandering** — no sect backing (Travelers, lone wolves)

## Martial Levels

Personal strength ladder (with in-between grades — low / mid / high):

**Martial Disciple** → **Martial Master** → **Martial Grandmaster** → **Transcendent**

- Only the very top of the top reaches Transcendent
- Travelers do not use this system — they have no martial level
