**The Shadow Creature**

**Identity:** A severed fragment of the devil god that the kingdom made a pact with.

**Origin:**

Pranav's parents are not from Earth. They were originally from another world — a kingdom that made a pact with an evil entity, a devil god. In exchange for help conquering the world, the kingdom would sacrifice its most beautiful (read: most magically potent) princess from each generation.

Pranav's mother was meant to be the next sacrifice. Her father, a Storymaker (one who travels between worlds as entertainment), gathered enough points to teleport both of them to Earth. The devil god's contract was violated. Half the kingdom's population died in the ensuing rampage.

Pranav was born with the indicator — two different colored eyes. The sacrifice mark. Her parents, as a precaution, cast an intricate curse to hide her features. To make her ugly. To keep her safe.

The kingdom eventually succeeded in developing a reverse summoning spell. They targeted the princess. But Pranav, being both the princess's daughter and of the bloodline, was the most potent elixir — the younger the princess, the purer the mana.

The reverse summoning triggered. The devil god, wanting to ensure success, ripped off a part of itself and sent it through the portal. Pranav's parents fought it off — but could not stop the summoning. They sacrificed themselves by replacing Pranav as the target.

**The severing:**

When the spell went off, the fragment of the devil god had its connection to the original body fully severed. It was stranded. Alone. Broken.

Its remaining instructions were fragmented: *ensure Pranav is safely something to something.* The rest was blank. It barely survived the severing. It no longer knew what it was.

**What it does:**

It saved Pranav from the fire — a side effect of the reverse summoning. With no strength, no memory, no sense of self, it hid within Pranav to regain power while following its only remaining command: *keep her safe.*

Its understanding of danger is broken. It cannot distinguish between a real threat and a perceived one. The uncle who made her uncomfortable. The bully who got too close. The dark at the edge of her room. It lashes out at anything it interprets as a threat — which has made its protection look exactly like a haunting.

**Current state:**

- Cannot communicate (still too weakened, still too fragmented)
- Became fully manifested the first time Pranav dropped to 0 HP (the failsafe triggered)
- Still does not know what it is, what it was, or what it was supposed to do with Pranav
- The original devil god may or may not know the fragment survived
- If the fragment ever regains enough power or memory — it may remember its original purpose
