**Setting the Scene**

The road takes them up. Not dramatically — just the gradual incline of terrain that has been rising for the last hour without the players necessarily noticing. Then the path crests and they are on a clifftop and the Church is below them in the valley and they can see all of it at once.

Stop here. Don't move them forward yet. Just describe what they see from up there.

The Church is gone. From this distance the full shape of the destruction is visible in a way it wouldn't be up close — the collapsed towers, the blackened stone, the specific stillness of somewhere that used to have constant movement and now has none. The smoke has mostly died down. What's left is just the fact of it, laid out below them, and it is total.

Give the players a moment on the clifftop before anything else happens. They can see it. They know what they are looking at. Let them sit with it before they have to go down into it.

---

**Arriving**

The descent from the clifftop takes them closer in increments. The destruction that looked total from above reveals its details on the way down — and the details are worse than the overview.

The first body is on the road before they even reach the gates. A Church soldier, face down, killed while running. Then another. Then two more just inside what used to be the outer wall. The pattern is consistent and grim — the further in the players go the more bodies there are, clustered and concentrated like the fighting collapsed inward as it ended. Whatever happened here pushed everyone toward the center and finished them there.

The inner sanctum is where it is worst. A last stand that was never going to hold — Church soldiers in a tight cluster, wounds consistent with something far beyond what they could have stopped. They held as long as they could. It wasn't long enough.

Players who keep moving through the ruins, past the inner sanctum, through the ash and the silence and the bodies, will eventually find Brother Aldric. Not dramatically — he is simply there, sitting against a collapsed wall with his back to the stone and his eyes open, watching them approach. He has been here long enough that he heard them coming from a distance and decided not to move. He is old, injured, and has been alone with all of this long enough that finding another person has briefly broken something loose in his composure. He pulls it back together quickly. He has something to say and he is going to say it.

---

**Brother Aldric**

Not a soldier — a record keeper, a man whose entire career has been documentation and administration. He tells them what happened plainly, without embellishment, in the way of someone who has already processed the grief and arrived somewhere quieter on the other side.

A demon force, larger and more organized than anything the Church had seen, hit without warning in the early hours. It was not a raid. It was an execution. The Church's leadership, its senior clergy, its command structure — all of it targeted specifically and systematically. Whoever sent this force knew exactly where to hit and in what order.

He does not know who survives in any official capacity. The Church as an institution is gone.

What he does know are two things:

**[[dnd/DM Sheet/ABCD/World 1/Characters/John/John (Act 3)|John]] and [[dnd/DM Sheet/ABCD/World 1/Characters/Luna/Luna (Act 3)|Luna]]'s group** — he received word before the attack. They are alive, operating somewhere to the east, a Church remnant holding together through sheer will. He has a rough location.

**The Hero Party** — also alive. Moving in the opposite direction, to the west. He has heard they are pushing toward demon territory directly. He does not say this with admiration.

He gives both locations. Then he leans back against the wall and closes his eyes. He is not dead — he is just done. He has passed on what he had. The rest is the players' problem now.

---

**The Choice**

Two directions. No right answer. No one to tell them which matters more.

The Hero Party to the west — moving aggressively, powerful enough to cause damage, not nearly powerful enough to win. Arrogant enough not to know the difference.

John and Luna to the east — a remnant, diminished, still moving for reasons that are entirely personal now that the institution behind them is ash.

Players choose. Do not push them. Do not hint. Just let the silence after Aldric finishes sit until someone speaks.

**Point Moments (DM Only)**

- A player's reaction on the clifftop — before they descend, just from the sight of it
- A player notices the first body on the road and stops — reward whoever clocks it first
- A player searches the inner sanctum and takes in the last stand without saying anything
- A player asks Aldric if anything was worth saving from inside — let him think about it before he answers
- A player asks Aldric what he is going to do now — he doesn't have a good answer and doesn't pretend to
- The moment the party realizes they have to choose a direction and nobody speaks for a beat too long
