- **Role:** Frontline Tank, Shield Bearer
- **Background:** Second son of a military noble house — a family that has produced generals and decorated soldiers for three generations. He did not inherit the title or the estate, that went to his older brother, but he inherited the family's absolute certainty that their blood produces the finest fighters the world has to offer. Every opponent he has ever faced has been weaker than him. He has never fought anyone stronger. His entire identity is built on that unbroken foundation and the Holt family name behind it.
- **Personality:** Loud, physical, takes up space deliberately. The arrogance is bodily and inherited in equal measure — he trusts his body completely and trusts his bloodline's legacy even more. Has never been given a reason to question either. Assumes anyone who claims to be stronger is either lying or hasn't met a Holt yet. Gets along with Kael naturally — two nobles who have decided the world confirms everything they already believed about themselves.
- **Opinion of players:** Sizes them up physically the moment he meets them and makes a judgment he will not revise until something forces him to.
- **Fighting Style:** Plants himself and dares things to move him. Uses his shield as a weapon as much as protection. Hits harder than someone his size should and knows it.
- **CR:** 7
