_Location: 1-B — The direct but dangerous path._

---

**Bandit Stronghold**

A fortified position carved into a rocky hillside overlooking the main road. Wooden palisades, watchtowers, a gate that stays closed unless the sentries decide otherwise. Well-supplied and well-organized — these aren't starving criminals. They've been here long enough to build something.

---

**The Bandits**

- Large enough that neither the Church nor the Demon King's forces have bothered clearing them out.
- Pragmatic, not cruel. They take what they need but don't kill without reason.
- Their leader is calculating — they know the war keeps travelers desperate, and desperate people pay more.

---

**Approach Options**

| Approach | Likely Outcome |
|---|---|
| **Fight through** | Hard. Fortified position, numbers advantage, prepared defenses. Costly. |
| **Negotiate passage** | They'll demand payment — coin, supplies, or information. Price depends on what you have. |
| **Sneak past** | Possible if the party is careful. The sentries are good, not perfect. |
| **Hire them** | For the right price, they might escort you through their territory instead of fighting you. |

---

**Potential Hooks**

- A recent captive is being held in the stronghold — someone the players might know or have reason to free.
- The bandits intercepted a Church supply shipment. Among the supplies is something the players need.
- The bandit leader knows something about the road ahead and is willing to trade that information.
