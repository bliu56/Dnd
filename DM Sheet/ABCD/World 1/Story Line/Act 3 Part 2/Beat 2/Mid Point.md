_Location: Mid Point — First converge. Both paths from the first two locations lead here._

---

**The Intelligence Outpost**

A fortified stone structure built into a low ridge. Originally a Church intelligence outpost — scouts, messengers, signal relays, and a small garrison to protect them. Two weeks ago, a demon patrol found it. The garrison didn't last the night.

The demons have repurposed it as a forward observation post. No reinforcements have arrived yet — whoever was supposed to relieve them probably hasn't heard the outpost fell.

---

**Current Occupants**

Roughly a dozen demons, still settling in. They're not fully organized yet — some are cataloguing captured documents, some are securing the perimeter, some are just resting after the assault. They outnumber the party but aren't expecting a fight.

---

**The Structure**

| Area | Description |
|------|-------------|
| **Main Hall** | Former barracks. Now a staging area. Maps spread across tables, supply crates, a few cots. |
| **Communications Room** | Signal equipment — mirrors, flags, message tubes. Still intact. The demons haven't figured out how to use it yet. |
| **Commander's Office** | Locked (DC 15 thieves' tools or DC 18 Strength). Contains the outpost's original operational orders. |
| **Armoury** | Picked clean by the demons. Most weapons and armor have been moved to the main hall. |
| **Cellar** | Used for storage. The demons haven't searched it thoroughly. |
