_Location: 1-B — The direct but dangerous path._

---

**Bandit Camp**

Not a stronghold. A makeshift camp hidden in the hills — repurposed farm carts, lean-tos, a half-collapsed barn they've turned into shelter. These aren't career criminals. They're farmers, traders, craftspeople who lost everything when the war rolled through. The road below is the only way they know how to survive now.

Maybe 15-20 people total. Families mixed in with the fighters. They don't kill — they surround, intimidate, take what they need, and let people go. They're not good at this. They've only been doing it for a few months.

---

**NPCs**

**Tomas** — The reluctant leader.

A former farmer. His village was burned six months ago by a Church patrol hunting "demon sympathizers." His wife didn't make it. He gathered the survivors and led them into the hills because no one else would. He doesn't want to be here. He doesn't want to be robbing travelers. But the alternative was watching everyone starve.

He negotiates with a tired, flat voice. He doesn't threaten. He states facts. He hates every conversation he has to have.

> *"I don't want your money. I want you to go a different way so I don't have to take it."*

---

**Elara** — The camp's healer.

A midwife and herbalist before the war. She keeps the camp alive — treats injuries from failed robberies, delivers babies, tends to the elderly. She's seen the worst of what desperation does to people. She's the one who stitches up travelers who resist too hard.

She does not approve of what they've become. But she stays because if she leaves, people die.

> *"Don't look at me like that. I didn't choose this life. None of us did."*

---

**Dorian** — The one who's too good at this.

Late teens. He was a carpenter's apprentice before the war. Now he's one of the ones who stands in the road with a weapon and looks dangerous enough that travelers hand over their goods without a fight. He's discovered he's naturally intimidating — and he hates that about himself.

He volunteers for every job because he feels responsible for the families in the camp. He doesn't sleep well.

> *"I know what we look like. But there are children here. What would you do?"*

---

**Old Marren** — The memory of before.

Elderly. Her grandchildren are in the camp. She's too old to fight, so she cooks and mends and watches the little ones. She's the closest thing the camp has to a moral compass — not because she judges anyone, but because she reminds them what they're fighting for.

She tells old stories by the fire. From before the war. She's the only one who still talks about the future like it exists.

> *"I've survived two wars. I never thought I'd have to survive a third by becoming someone I don't recognize."*

---

**Lena and her child** — The reason.

A young mother. Her husband was conscripted by the Church and never came back. She has a child — maybe four or five — who doesn't fully understand what's happening. The child plays in the dirt near the camp's edge, unaware that their survival depends on strangers being scared enough to give up their food.

The camp's protectiveness of this child is what keeps everyone from crossing the line into真正的violence. As long as there's a child here, they're still people.

---

**Approach Options**

| Approach | Likely Outcome |
|---|---|
| **Fight through** | Possible but ugly. They're not good fighters, but they're desperate. Someone could die — on either side. |
| **Negotiate passage** | They'll ask for supplies or medicine. If you help someone in the camp (Elara's patients, the child), Tomas will let you through without taking anything. |
| **Sneak past** | Easy. Their sentries are exhausted farmers, not professional guards. |
| **Threaten them** | Works — but Tomas will let you through with a look that says he won't forget your face. |

---

**Potential Hooks**

- **Someone is badly injured** — a recent robbery went wrong, and one of the bandits is dying. Elara doesn't have the supplies. If the players help, the camp is grateful. Safe passage, maybe more.
- **They recognize a Church sigil** — if a player is wearing Church colors or symbols, the camp goes cold. Someone here was wronged by the Church directly.
- **They know the road ahead** — these people fled through this entire region. They know what's between here and the Mid Point better than anyone. They'll share what they know for fair trade.
- **Lena's child is sick** — nothing serious, but without medicine it could turn bad. A player with healing or medicine can earn the camp's trust instantly.

---

**If the Hero Party encounters them:**

- Gareth would probably respect the desperation. Wouldn't attack unprovoked.
- Isolde would judge them harshly — thieves are thieves, regardless of circumstance.
- Seraphine would analyze the situation and conclude they're not a threat worth engaging.
- Kael wouldn't even slow down. They're beneath his notice.
