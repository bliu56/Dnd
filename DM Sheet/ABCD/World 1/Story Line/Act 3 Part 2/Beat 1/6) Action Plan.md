**The Route to Ashen Vale**

From camp, there are multiple paths. They do not know what lies ahead at each fork — only that each choice leads somewhere different.

```
STARTING CAMP
    |
    +-- 1-A: Twin-Moon Lake  -->  2-A: Stillwater Hollow  --+
    |                                                        |
    +-- 1-B: Bandit Stronghold -->  2-B: Trading Outpost   --+
                                                             |
                                                         MID POINT
                                                             |
    +-- 3-A: Fallowmere  -->  4-A: Corrupted Treant's Lair -+
    |                                                        |
    +-- 3-B: Hearthfen   -->  4-B: Healer's Hut            -+
                                                             |
                                                         TWIN PEAKS
                                                             |
    +-- 5-A: Demon Encampment --+
    |                            |
    +-- 5-B: Rogue Demon Cell  -+
                                |
                            ASHEN VALE
```

**The flow:**
- Camp → choose **1-A (Twin-Moon Lake)** or **1-B (Bandit Stronghold)**
- After first location, choose **2-A (Stillwater Hollow)** or **2-B (Trading Outpost)**
- Both converge at **Mid Point** (TBD by DM)
- Mid Point → choose **3-A (Fallowmere)** or **3-B (Hearthfen)**
- Choose **4-A (Corrupted Treant's Lair)** or **4-B (Healer's Hut)**
- Both converge at **Twin Peaks**
- Twin Peaks → choose **5-A (Demon Encampment)** or **5-B (Rogue Demon Cell)**
- Arrive at **Ashen Vale**

**Total decision points: 5**
**Total locations visited: 5**
**Guaranteed stops: Mid Point, Twin Peaks**

**Location Details**

**1-A — Twin-Moon Lake**
- A large, still lake. Home to a pair of identical fish — **Lux** and **Umbra**. One grants life, one takes it. You cannot tell them apart until you eat one. People don't risk it.

**1-B — Bandit Stronghold**
- A fortified position overlooking a key stretch of road. Not aligned with Church or demons — they take from whoever passes.

**2-A — Stillwater Hollow**
- A village built around a spring-fed pool. At the center of the pool, a sword is embedded in stone — **The Blade at the Stillwater**. It chooses its wielder at random.
- **Kaelen** — a silver-white spirit fish. Oldest thing in the village. Surfaces only when the sword is about to speak.
- The **spirit of the blade** manifests as a woman's form above the water. Speaks in fragments of forgotten things.

**2-B — Trading Outpost**
- A survivor outpost. Humans only. Everyone here lost their home to the war. Slightly more resentful toward the Church than toward demons.

- (TBD by DM)

**3-A — Fallowmere**
- A village that the Church abandoned long ago. When Church soldiers returned to take resources by force, the villagers sided with demons who had been secretly helping them. A villager died trying to protect a demon. Now the villagers and demons are fighting together against the Church encampment outside the village.

**3-B — Hearthfen**
- A human village with a secret. The village chief's daughter was not kidnapped by a demon — she ran away with one. They are in love. The chief calls it a kidnapping anyway.

**4-A — Corrupted Treant's Lair**
- A forest guardian driven mad by the war. The demon army has been trying to kill it for its core — a dense source of corrupted natural magic. The area around the lair is twisted: thorn walls, rotting ground, animals acting wrong.

**4-B — Healer's Hut**
- A remote hut. The healer is ancient and treats everyone — Church soldiers, civilians, and demons alike. No questions asked. No side taken.

**Twin Peaks**
- The 3/4 mark. All paths converge here. (To be detailed)

**5-A — Demon Encampment**
- Not a military post. Just demons living their lives. Families, traders, people trying to survive the same war everyone else is.

**5-B — Rogue Demon Cell**
- A splinter group of demons that reject the Demon King's rule. They're cruel, violent, and indiscriminate. Hunted by both sides.
