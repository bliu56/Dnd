_Scene: Gareth's morning strength training. He's been up before sunrise._

---

Gareth is already away from the main camp when the players stir. They find him stripped down to his undershirt, working through a routine with a fallen log. Controlled. Heavy. Rhythmic. He's not showing off — this is just what he does in the morning.

He notices them approaching. Doesn't stop.

**Gareth:** "Morning. You're up early. Good."

A grunt. He resets and goes again.

**Gareth:** "I don't do sitting around. If you're gonna stand there, grab something heavy and make yourself useful. I wanna see what you're made of before we head out."

Not mean. Just direct. He genuinely wants to know.

---

**The Routine**

Three exercises. The player can attempt any or all of them. Gareth does them alongside — he's not competing, he's just setting the pace. The rolls represent how well the player keeps up.

| Exercise | Ability Check |
|---|---|
| **The Lift** — Lift a heavy log or stone from ground to chest, hold, and set down | Athletics (STR) |
| **The Carry** — Loaded with weight, walk a marked distance and back without stopping | Constitution check |
| **The Brace** — Stand your ground while Gareth pushes against you with his full weight | Athletics (STR) or Strength check (player's choice) |

| Roll | Outcome | Gareth's reaction |
|---|---|---|
| **10 or below** | Fail. Can't complete it or have to stop early. | "That's it? I've seen toddlers do better. Again. Don't stop until you finish." |
| **11–15** | Pass. Complete it cleanly. Nothing impressive. | "Decent. But decent isn't going to keep you alive. Push harder next time." |
| **16+** | Excel. Smooth, controlled, catches his attention. | A pause. He looks at you like he's re-evaluating. "...Alright. That's more like it. But don't let it go to your head. One good rep doesn't make a warrior." |
| **20** | Perfect execution. He stops. | Silence. He stares at you for a long moment. Then he cracks the smallest grin. "...Name. I want your name." That's all. He goes back to his routine, but he won't forget it. |

**Gareth:** (if anyone hesitates or looks tired) "Rest when the fight's over. Right now you're still breathing, which means you can still move. Move."

---

**If a player joins him:**

He glances at them. Short nod. No smile. But he notices.

**Gareth:** "Alright. Let's see it. Keep up if you can."

He adjusts the weight so they can share or alternate. He doesn't coach — just does his routine and expects them to follow. How they handle it tells him everything.

**If no one joins:**

He shrugs.

**Gareth:** "Suit yourself. You'll have to show me on the road eventually."

He keeps going. He'll be watching them during the journey with more interest than he'd admit.

---

**Tone Notes (DM Only)**

- Gareth isn't cruel — he's direct. The harshness is because he believes everyone can be stronger, and he's not going to coddle them into it.
- If a player fails, giving them a chance to retry (with disadvantage or a higher DC) is fine. Gareth respects persistence more than raw talent.
- The "Name. I want your name." moment at a natural 20 is the highest compliment he can give. He won't say anything nice about it after — he just remembers.
