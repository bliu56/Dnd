_Scene: Isolde returns from morning prayers and greets the party._

---

Isolde emerges from wherever she went — returned from her morning prayers. She's the first of the Hero Party to come back to camp.

**Isolde:** (warm, bright) "Oh, you're awake! Good morning, good morning."

She settles near the fire, accepts a cup from Maren with a thank you, and then — without another word — closes her eyes. The warmth on her face settles into something quieter. Her lips move silently. A handful of seconds. Stillness.

Then she opens her eyes, breathes out, and the smile returns.

**Isolde:** (to the players) "Would you like to join me? Just for a moment. It doesn't have to be long. The Goddess listens to any voice that speaks to her — she doesn't mind if you're still figuring out what to say."

She doesn't push. Just holds the space open, warm, and waits.

**If a player joins her:**

She doesn't open her eyes, but a small smile crosses her face. She waits a moment — letting them settle — and then speaks softly:

> *"Aelura, who sees what is hidden and holds what is broken: we come to you this morning with open hands. We do not ask for greatness, or for the weight of the world to lift. Only that you walk beside us in the hours ahead, steady our feet when the path is unclear, and remind us that even in shadow — we are not forgotten."*

A pause. Then, quieter:

> *"And if there is something they cannot yet speak — hold it for them until they are ready."*

She opens her eyes and looks at whoever joined her with a warmth that is entirely unguarded.

**Isolde:** "Thank you. The Goddess smiles upon our journey."

---

**After the prayer:**

**Isolde:** (standing, brushing off her robes) "Now then. Let me take a look at you."

She moves to each player in turn. Not clinical — gentle. She takes their hands, or places a hand on their shoulder, or simply looks at them with that same unhurried warmth. But something shifts in her eyes — like she's reading something just beneath the surface.

**To Connor:** (a soft smile) "You carry weight that isn't yours. Deadlines. Expectations. Someone who doesn't see how hard you're working — and a mirror that tells you it's not enough anyway. The Goddess sees you burning yourself out for people who won't notice. You don't have to prove anything today."

**To Bowen:** (tilting her head, voice softer) "Someone is missing. Someone you're waiting for. The silence in your house is louder than anything else right now. You're trying to hold things together. That's not a burden you were meant to carry alone."

**To Pranav:** (a long, searching look — something flickers in her expression, uncertainty) "You've been followed for a very long time. Since you were small. By something that does not belong to the light. I cannot see it clearly — it hides from me — but I can feel the cold it left behind. You are still standing, but you are not alone. The Goddess sees you."

**To Andrew:** (warm, gentle) "There is anger in you. Quiet, banked, waiting. Someone hurt someone you love. A choice. You're still carrying the weight of what you didn't do." (a pause) "The Goddess does not ask you to forgive. She only asks you not to let the anger decide who you become."

**To Chris:** (a soft look, almost sad) "You're watching someone. Waiting for them to come back. There's a door you haven't opened yet — and a room that holds more than you're ready to face. You're stronger than you think you are. The fact that you're still sitting up — the Goddess sees you."

She stops in front of **Darrin**.

The warmth drains out of her face in an instant. Her hand, which had been reaching out, stops mid-air. Her eyes go cold.

**Isolde:** (flat, quiet) "...."

She pulls her hand back like she touched something hot.

She turns away from Darrin without another word. The warmth doesn't return to her voice for the rest of the exchange.
