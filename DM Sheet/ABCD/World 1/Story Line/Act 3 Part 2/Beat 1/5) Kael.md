_Scene: Kael is the last to emerge. The players do not exist to him yet._

---

Kael is the last of the Hero Party to emerge. The fire is going. Breakfast is ready. The morning routines are winding down.

He steps out of his tent and surveys the camp. His eyes pass over the players like they're part of the scenery. No pause. No evaluation. They're just... there.

He walks to the fire. Finn hands him a plate. He takes it. Sits. Eats.

A few moments of silence. Then, without looking at the players:

**Kael:** "Finn. Make sure they're fed and ready before we move. I don't want to wait for anyone."

That's it. He hasn't acknowledged them directly. He's spoken about them, not to them. They are logistical considerations — like supplies, like horses, like anything else that needs to be managed before the Hero can do his work.

He finishes his plate, sets it down, and stands.

**Kael:** (to Finn, still not looking at the players) "I'll be reviewing the route. Come find me when everyone's ready."

He walks off. The conversation about the route and the plan will happen — but he expects to be briefed, not consulted.

---

**If a player tries to talk to him directly:**

He pauses. Looks at them for the first time. The look is not hostile — it's just flat. Like a piece of furniture made a sound.

**Kael:** "Did I ask you something?"

Beat.

**Kael:** "Then we have nothing to discuss."

He continues walking.

---

**If a player tries to challenge or prove themselves:**

He stops. Turns. Looks at them properly for the first time.

**Kael:** "I don't know who you think you are. But I am the Hero. You are here because I allowed it. Don't mistake that for us being equals."

He holds the gaze just long enough to make the point, then turns and walks away. He does not look back. The conversation is over.

---

**Tone Notes (DM Only)**

- Kael genuinely does not see the players as worth engaging with yet. He will speak about them, not to them.
- The only thing that could change this is actions, not words. A player who does something impressive in combat or decision-making might earn a single sentence of acknowledgment later.
- Until then, he will route all communication through Finn, Maren, or another Hero Party member. The players are, to him, hired help.
