_Type: Morning / Tone Setting / Introduction_

**The Morning**

The camp is quiet. The fire burned down to embers sometime in the night.

The players wake first — or at least, some of them do. The Hero Party is still asleep. Kael has his back against a tree, eyes closed, looking like he's been posed there. Gareth is sprawled out like a fallen statue. Seraphine is curled in her cloak, barely visible. Isolde is already gone from her spot.

Two figures are moving around the campfire, quiet and practiced.

Finn is stoking the embers back to life. Maren is unpacking rations from a satchel. They notice the players stirring.

---

**Finn and Maren**

Finn is the attendant who moves with deference — a slight bow in his posture, careful with every word. Maren is the one who gets things done without ceremony, practical and unbothered.

**Finn:** (bows head slightly) "Good morning. I trust you rested well. Before we prepare the morning meal — do any of you have dietary restrictions we should account for? It is no trouble at all."

**Maren:** (already busy with the fire, doesn't look up) "Yeah what he said. We've got enough to work around pretty much anything, just tell us before the pot's on."

Beat.

**Maren:** "'Cause the Hero Party? Oh they're *picky*. Kael won't touch anything that wasn't prepared properly — has to be hot, has to be plated right, like we're running a tavern. Gareth just wants *volume*, doesn't care what it is as long as there's enough of it. Seraphine eats whatever's fastest, doesn't even look at the plate half the time. And Isolde..." (she lowers her voice) "— She prays over every bite. Quietly. Takes forever. We just learned to serve her first."

**Finn:** (mildly) "Maren."

**Maren:** "What? They asked."

---

**Tone Notes (DM Only)**

- This is the players' first real interaction with anyone from the Hero Party's camp. Finn and Maren are the gate — approachable, neutral, not judgmental.
- Finn's respect is genuine, not performative. He will answer questions honestly but won't gossip about the Hero Party unless pressed.
- Maren's casual tone is also genuine. She talks because she doesn't see the point in not talking. If players engage her, she'll keep going.
- The dietary details serve two purposes: (1) makes the Hero Party feel like real people with quirks, and (2) gives players an easy, low-stakes way to engage with the camp before the tension ramps up.
- Let players set the pace here. The Hero Party will wake when it's time.
