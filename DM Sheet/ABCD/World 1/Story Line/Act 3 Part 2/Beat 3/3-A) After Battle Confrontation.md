_Scene: Immediately after the Margrove fight. The players are catching their breath, frustrated that the Hero Party barely helped._

---

**The Truth — The Hero Party's Perspective**

The Hero Party did not abandon the fight. They were never going to.

From the moment the player party joined them, the Hero Party has been running a deliberate, coordinated evaluation. They don't trust strangers — not in a war where demons can wear any face, where a single planted spy can undo months of work. The player party approached *them* with good information, but that doesn't mean they are who they claim to be. They could be demons in disguise. Contracted agents. Demon worshippers. Sympathizers. The Hero Party has seen too much to take anyone at face value.

So they tested them.

---

**The Tests**

**1. The Watch — Did they sleep normally?**
Hero Party members took turns watching through the night. Everyone slept normally — except Darrin.
- Darrin's sleep was restless. He woke multiple times. Muttered things they couldn't quite catch.
- Not damning on its own. Strange people have strange nights. But noted.

**2. The Soup — Would they eat blessed food?**
Before the morning workout, Finn blessed the breakfast stew under Isolde's instruction. Anyone with ill intent or demonic connection would find it repulsive or react negatively.
- Everyone ate normally — except Darrin. He refused the pork stew entirely.
- *"Not hungry."* A reasonable excuse. But paired with the restless night? Suspicious.

**3. Isolde's Intuition — Touch and revelation.**
Isolde's holy sensitivity is not infallible, but it has never been wrong about *evil*. When she touched the players, she saw:
- Honest struggles. Real pain. Flawed but genuine people.
- Then she reached Darrin — and recoiled. Something dark. Something that clung to him like oil on water. She could not see through it, but she felt it clearly: this was not clean. This was not the Goddess's domain.

**4. The Battle — How did he fight?**
Darrin's performance in Margrove gave the Hero Party everything they needed.

---

**The Evidence Against Darrin**

| What Happened | Why It Matters |
|---|---|
| Darrin used Seraphine's Shatter to perform a **Mega Shatter** | Mega Shatter is not a known spell. It does not exist in any record. And more importantly — **Seraphine felt it.** When Darrin pulled the spell from her, she felt something *wrong*. A cold thread, unnatural, sliding into her magic and twisting it. That is not a normal caster's ability. |
| The blood demon (Caelen) escaped toward the exact location of the children — the same area Darrin had positioned his crow earlier | Coincidence? Or did the crow guide the demon to its escape route? The crow was Darrin's. The demon ran straight to the one place the Hero Party couldn't pursue freely. |
| Darrin claimed there were **3 demons** inside | There were **5**. Two were hidden, waiting. If the Hero Party had followed the player party's intel, they would have been flanked. Darrin either made a critical mistake — or he knew about the hidden demons and chose not to share. |

---

**The Conclusion**

The player party seems genuine. Connor, Bowen, Pranav, Andrew, Chris — flawed, real, carrying real burdens. They are not the threat.

**Darrin is the problem.**

The Hero Party has reached a single conclusion: Darrin is a spy. Infiltrated the group. Feeding misinformation. Positioning himself to control the party's movements and decisions. The restless sleep. The refusal of blessed food. Isolde's recoil. The unexplained magic. The false intel. The demon escaping to exactly where Darrin's eyes were.

It is not a coincidence. It is a pattern.

The Hero Party does not know what Darrin's true goals are. They do not know who he answers to. But they know one thing for certain:

**He cannot be allowed to continue with this group.**
