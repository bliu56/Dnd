_Scene: Ambush on the road, shortly after leaving the Mid Point._

---

**Trigger:** The party is on the road, heading toward their next destination. The Hero Party is with them. Tempers are still raw from the Darrin confrontation, but the group is moving.

---

### Phase 1 — The Ambush

The road ahead is quiet. Too quiet. Before anyone can call it out, a lance of blood-red energy streaks from the treeline — aimed at the nearest member of the player party.

**Caelen, the Bleeding Hand,** emerges from the undergrowth. His sigil cloak is torn. His eyes are wild. He is not calculating. He is *desperate.*

He doesn't speak. He attacks.

**Why:** Caelen's base — the Mid Point intelligence outpost — was hit. He returned to find it destroyed. His squad is dead. He saw the Hero Party's banners from a distance and assumed the worst: Ash is captured or dead, and the people who killed his friends are marching triumphantly down the road.

He is not trying to win. He is buying time for Ash — buying it with his life if he has to.

---

### Phase 2 — Intervention

Before anyone lands a killing blow, one of the Hero Party (or a player) recognizes what's happening.

**Seraphine:** (sharp) *"Wait. That's Caelen. He's one of Ash's — he's not supposed to be here."*

**Kael:** *"He's attacking us. That's all I need to know."*

**Isolde:** (stepping forward, hands open) *"Stop. Look at him. He's not trying to win. He's trying to die."*

A DC 13 Insight check confirms it — Caelen's stance is not an offensive stance. It's a desperate rearguard. He expects to die. He's fine with it as long as it buys time.

**If the players call for a halt or try to talk him down,** the Hero Party hesitates. Kael is the hardest to convince — he sees a threat and wants it removed. But if the players make a compelling case, even he will hold.

---

### Phase 3 — The Breakdown

Once Caelen realizes he's not being immediately killed, and that the people in front of him are not what he expected, his composure cracks.

**Caelen:** *"Where is Ash?! What did you do with her?!"*

**If told Ash is alive and coordinating with the party:**

He freezes. His weapon lowers an inch.

**Caelen:** *"...What?"*

**If told the Mid Point was cleared by the party — that the demons there were enemies:**

His face twists. Not in anger — in grief.

**Caelen:** *"Enemies. You call them enemies. You don't even know their names. You don't know what they were before — what we all were before he found us."*

He straightens. His voice rises — not a threat, but a wound finally opening.

**Caelen:** *"We were slaves. Every single one of us. Chained, branded, sold. Demon flesh is valuable, you know — makes fine ingredients for your magic. We were bred like cattle, harvested alive, piece by piece, until there was nothing left worth keeping. And no one cared. Because we were 'demons.'"*

He laughs — bitter, wet.

**Caelen:** *"The Church didn't protect us. The humans didn't shelter us. We were worth more dead than alive — and everyone knew it. Our population is so low they list us as endangered. Endangered. Like we're some kind of animal."*

He points at the Hero Party.

**Caelen:** *"And you fight for them. The people who let this happen. Who stood by while we were torn apart for spell components and status symbols."*

**His voice drops.**

**Caelen:** *"The Demon King was the first one who didn't see us as currency. He gave us power so we could fight back. So we could live without looking over our shoulders. And you — you killed my friends because they were in your way."*

---

### Phase 4 — Resolution

**If asked about the bodies at the Mid Point:**

Caelen's jaw tightens.

**Caelen:** *"Demon parts have always been good ingredients. They didn't do anything to deserve what was done to them. We only ever killed people who deserved it — unlike your kind."*

He looks away.

**Caelen:** *"I need to go back. I need to find what's left of them. Give them a proper burning. That's the least they deserve."*

He doesn't ask for permission. He states it.

---

### Outcomes

| Path | What happens |
|------|-------------|
| **Let him go** | Caelen leaves to recover his squad's bodies. He may appear again later — his loyalty to Ash and the Demon King is absolute. |
| **Offer help** | If the party offers to help retrieve the bodies, Caelen is visibly thrown. He doesn't trust it, but he doesn't refuse. This could earn genuine goodwill — or suspicion. |
| **Attack / detain him** | Caelen fights to the death. He will not be taken alive. If killed, Ash learns of it eventually, and the party's relationship with the demons is permanently damaged. |
| **Bring him along** | He refuses. His duty is to his dead. But he gives the party a token — a blood-red shard from his cloak. *"Show that to any demon you meet on the road. It means you're not to be touched. It buys you one conversation."* |

---

### What the Party Learns

- Demons were systematically enslaved, hunted, and harvested for body parts before the Demon King.
- Their population is critically low — endangered species status.
- The Demon King freed them and gave them the power to defend themselves.
- Caelen and Ash's squad were not soldiers of conquest. They were former slaves given a second chance.
- The Mid Point demons were not "evil" — they were people trying to survive.

---

### Hero Party Reactions

| Member | Reaction |
|--------|----------|
| **Kael** | Says nothing during Caelen's speech. His expression is unreadable. After Caelen leaves, he walks ahead without a word. He does not comment on what was said. |
| **Isolde** | Listens. Her face is pale. She does not interrupt. When Caelen mentions the Church, she flinches — barely. She does not defend it. |
| **Seraphine** | Takes notes. Quietly. When Caelen mentions demon parts being used as ingredients, she pauses — pen hovering over the page. She does not write that part down. |
| **Gareth** | Watches Caelen the whole time. His hand is on his weapon until Caelen turns to leave. Then he lets it drop. He says: *"That one's got more理由 than most."* |

---

### DM Notes

- This scene is the party's first real glimpse into the demon perspective. Up until now, demons have been enemies. This is where the moral complexity of the war opens up.
- Let Caelen talk. Don't rush him. His grief is genuine, and his anger is earned. Players who try to argue with him will find he has answers for every point.
- The token (if given) is a significant resource. It signals to demons that the party is under Ash's protection — or at least, worth listening to before attacking.
- This scene works best if it ends quietly. No big battle music. Just a man walking back toward the bodies of his friends.
