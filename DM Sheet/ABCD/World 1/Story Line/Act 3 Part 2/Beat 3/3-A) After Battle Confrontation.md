_Scene: Immediately after the Margrove fight._

---

**Phase 1 — The Signal**

The Margrove fight is over. Exhaustion. Silence. The Hero Party is scattered — not postured, not ready.

Kael, from across the clearing, quiet:

> "Do it now."

**DM roll:** Kael rolls **Stealth** (muttered, under his breath). Players roll **Perception** to catch it.

**If a player hears it:** They have a brief window — one reaction — before Gareth moves.

**Gareth's attack:** Whether anyone reacts or not, Gareth commits. He closes the distance. One clean hit. Darrin goes down — prone, pinned, alive.

**Read after the hit:**

> *"Darrin is on the ground. Gareth has one knee on his back, one arm twisted behind him. Not dead. Not dying. But not getting up without help."*
>
> *"Isolde steps between you and them. Arms open — not hostile, protective. She's looking at *you* — the rest of the party — like she's begging you to understand."*
>
> *"Seraphine has repositioned. Flanking. Covering angles."*
>
> *"Kael hasn't moved. He's watching you. Waiting."*

---

**Phase 2 — The Standoff**

The players have options:

| If they... | Then... |
|---|---|
| Attack the Hero Party | Roll initiative. Hero Party fights to subdue, not kill. |
| Demand an explanation | Isolde speaks. See evidence below. |
| Stand down / listen | Hero Party explains. Relieved you're listening. |
| Try to heal / free Darrin | Gareth tightens the pin. Darrin takes 1d4 damage. |

---

Isolde or Seraphine lays out the case. One piece at a time. Players can interrupt, question, or argue at any point.

| Evidence | Who says it |

- The rest of the player party — Connor, Bowen, Pranav, Andrew, Chris — are genuine. Flawed but real.

- **He cannot be allowed to continue with this group.**
