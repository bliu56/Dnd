_Scene: The Mistweaver's fog separates the party from the Hero Party. When it clears — Caelen is there, and he's made a very wrong assumption._

---

## The Encounter

The fog rolls in thick and fast. The Mistweaver's work. Shouts are swallowed. Shapes vanish. The Hero Party is gone before anyone can react.

When the fog clears, the party is somewhere else. The road is gone. Margrove is gone. Just forest, silence — and **Caelen, the Bleeding Hand** standing at the treeline.

He's not here for the party. He's here for **Ash**.

Caelen sees Ash first. Then he sees the party standing with him. And he draws the wrong conclusion — that Ash is a hostage, a prisoner, or that the party has captured him. He moves to attack, targeting the party as the aggressors.

**The fight is a misunderstanding.** Caelen believes he's rescuing Ash. Ash knows this and may or may not correct him depending on how the party handles it.

| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Caelen's assumption** | The party has captured or hurt Ash. He attacks to free him. |
| **Ash's position** | Ash knows Caelen is wrong. He watches. He may clarify — or let it play out. |
| **The party's choice** | Fight Caelen, try to talk him down, or let Ash handle it. |

**If the party fights Caelen:** It's a real fight. Caelen is dangerous (CR 7). Ash stays neutral unless directly attacked.

**If they try to talk:** Caelen won't listen easily — he saw what he saw. A DC 18 Persuasion or Insight check to get through to him, or Ash can end it with a single sentence.

**If Ash intervenes:** One line from Ash clears it up. *"They're with me. Stand down."* Caelen stops immediately. He's confused but he listens to Ash.

---

## Aftermath

- If Caelen stands down, he may join the escort or peel off depending on Ash's orders.
- If Caelen is fought and driven off, he escapes — wounded but alive.
- Either way, the journey continues with Ash. He doesn't comment much on what just happened.
