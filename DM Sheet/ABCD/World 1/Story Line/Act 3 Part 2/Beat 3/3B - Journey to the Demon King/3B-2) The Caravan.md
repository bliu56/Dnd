_The party is traveling down the road with Ash when they spot a child under a tree off to the side._

---

## The Hook

The road stretches ahead. Quiet. Ash walks at a steady pace, unhurried, one hand resting near his blade out of habit. Then he stops. Listening.

Off to the side of the road, under the roots of an old tree, a child is curled up — maybe 8 or 9, scraped up, clothes torn, trying very hard to be quiet and failing.

They see the players and freeze. Too scared to run. Too scared to stay.

**The child's story:**
- Their caravan was heading east when Church soldiers appeared
- A man in charge — **Crane** — ordered them surrounded
- Emmett is there. His wife is there. Elderly. Other children.
- The child ran while the soldiers were forming up
- The caravan is just east of here, in an opening in the trees

Ash listens. His hand is no longer resting on his blade — it's gripping it.

---

## The Scene

The party crests the last ridge and sees it below:

An open clearing. A battered caravan stopped in the middle. Wagons, supplies scattered, people huddled together — Emmett standing at the front, arms out, trying to shield the others.

Around them: Church soldiers in a loose formation. Not attacking yet. Waiting.

**Crane** stands at the center of the line, giving quiet orders. His voice doesn't carry, but his posture is unmistakable — this is a man who has stopped negotiating.

Further back, at the treeline, a massive figure in plate armor stands motionless. Watching. **Sir Adonis**, the Paladin.

Ash watches Adonis for a moment, then speaks — low, to the party. He volunteers to take the Paladin, draw him off, keep him busy. He doesn't wait for debate. He's already moving.

Ash walks past the party, down the slope, directly toward Adonis. No rush. No weapon drawn. Adonis turns. They stare at each other. Then both move — not toward the caravan, but away from it, into the deeper tree line. The sounds of combat start a few moments later, distant and heavy.

The Church soldiers notice. Crane notices. His eyes find the party on the ridge. He draws his sword.

## The Fight

**Party vs. Crane + soldiers.** Standard combat encounter.

**Key elements:**
- Soldiers are positioned around the caravan — the party has to push through or split up
- Crane uses Tactical Command to direct his soldiers, making the fight harder if they're ignored
- The caravan people are non-combatants — the party needs to keep them out of the crossfire
- Ash vs Adonis is happening offscreen — sounds of combat, trees falling, then silence

**If Crane is taken out:**
- The soldiers waver. Without his command, they lose formation.
- Some may flee. Some may fight to the death depending on how deep Crane's influence runs.
- Adonis may return if he defeated Ash — or Ash may return, wounded or victorious.

---

## Notes (DM Only)

- The child from the hook is one of the caravan children. They'll run to their family once the fight starts.
- Crane's Martyrs' Reliquary makes him harder to kill while his soldiers are alive — the party should take out soldiers first, or focus Crane hard.
- Ash vs Adonis result is yours to decide. Either can win, draw, or be interrupted.
- Emmett will help stabilize wounded players after the fight if asked. He has basic healing supplies.
