**Saving Throws:** Wis +4, Cha +3 **Skills:** Medicine +6, Religion +3, Insight +4 **Senses:** Passive Perception 12 **Languages:** Common, Celestial

### Traits

**_Goddess's Ward._** The priestess has advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects from non-divine sources.

**_Spellcasting._** The priestess is a 3rd level divine spellcaster. Her spellcasting ability is Wisdom (spell save DC 12, +4 to hit). She has the following spells prepared:

- _Cantrips:_ Sacred Flame, Guidance, Spare the Dying
- _1st (3 slots):_ Healing Word, Cure Wounds, Bless
- _2nd (2 slots):_ Prayer of Healing, Aid

### Actions

**_Mace._** +1 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 1d6 bludgeoning + 1d4 radiant.

**_Sacred Flame._** DC 12 Dex save, range 60 ft. Fail: 1d8 radiant damage. Success: no damage.

**_Healing Word (3/Day)._** One ally within 60 ft. regains 1d4+2 HP as a bonus action.

### Reactions

**_Protective Prayer._** When an ally within 30 ft. is hit by an attack, the priestess can impose disadvantage on the attack roll as a reaction.
