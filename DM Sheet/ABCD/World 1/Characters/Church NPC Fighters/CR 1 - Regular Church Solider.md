### Traits

**_Formation Fighting._** While within 5 ft. of at least one allied soldier, this soldier has advantage on attack rolls against any creature that is also within 5 ft. of that ally.

**_Demoralized._** If this soldier starts its turn with no allied soldiers within 30 ft. and Crane is not conscious, it must make a DC 10 Wisdom save or use its movement to move toward the nearest ally rather than toward an enemy.

### Actions

**_Spear._** +4 to hit, reach 5 ft. or range 20/60 ft. Hit: 1d6+2 piercing.

**_Shield Bash._** +4 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 1d4+2 bludgeoning. The target must make a DC 12 Strength save or be knocked prone.

### Reactions

**_Brace._** When the soldier would be pushed or knocked prone, it can use its reaction to make a DC 12 Strength save. On a success the effect is negated.
