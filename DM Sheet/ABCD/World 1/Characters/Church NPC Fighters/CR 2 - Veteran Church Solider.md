### Traits

**_Hardened._** The veteran cannot be frightened while Crane is conscious. If Crane goes down, the veteran must make a DC 13 Wisdom save at the start of each turn or become frightened until the start of its next turn.

**_Experienced Fighter._** The veteran does not suffer disadvantage on attack rolls when prone and can stand up using only 5 ft. of movement instead of half their speed.

### Actions

**_Multiattack._** The veteran makes two Longsword attacks.

**_Longsword._** +5 to hit, reach 5 ft. Hit: 1d8+3 slashing.

**_Disarming Strike (1/Turn)._** When the veteran hits with a Longsword attack, the target must make a DC 13 Strength save or drop one held item of the veteran's choice.

### Bonus Actions

**_Aggressive._** The veteran moves up to its speed toward a hostile creature it can see.

### Reactions

**_Parry._** When hit by a melee attack, the veteran reduces the damage by 1d6+1.
