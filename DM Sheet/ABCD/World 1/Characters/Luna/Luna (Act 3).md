
- **Role:** Priest, All-Rounder Combat Cleric
- **Affiliation:** The Church
- **Status:** Alive
- **Age:** 19 (Act 3)

### Background

- Served under [[dnd/DM Sheet/ABCD/World 1/Characters/Michael|Michael]] as his student until his death at the end of Act 1
- Continued her career in the Church following Michael's passing, throwing herself into training
- No longer early in her career — two years of deliberate, grief-driven growth have made her formidable
- Traveled and fought alongside [[dnd/DM Sheet/ABCD/World 1/Characters/John/John (Act 3)|John]], developing into a true all-rounder: healing, support, damage, and defense in equal measure
- Still devoted to the Goddess and the Church's mission, though Michael's absence has quietly shifted how she carries that faith — it is more personal now, less institutional

### Personality

- Still happy-go-lucky on the surface — warm, upbeat, finds the bright side
- The cheerfulness is genuine but it sits on top of something heavier now that wasn't there before
- Michael's death left a mark she doesn't talk about directly but shows in the way she takes her work seriously
- Bickers with John as naturally as breathing — the dynamic shifted into something warmer and more settled without losing any of its edge
- Positive toward the party — genuinely glad they existed, genuinely unsettled that she hasn't seen or heard from them since Act 1
- Worries about them in the quiet way she worries about most things: steadily, without making it anyone else's problem

### Relationship with Michael's Memory

- Grieves him in the way a student grieves a mentor — not loudly, but constantly
- Became stronger partly because she felt she owed it to him
- Holds his example as the standard she measures herself against

### Relationship with John

- Together — what started as bickering and mutual exasperation became something neither of them planned for
- She is the reason his walls shifted. She is also fully aware of this and finds it quietly funny
- Their combat dynamic is deeply coordinated — she reads him better than anyone and he fights better because of it

### Relationship with the Party

- Positive, with an undercurrent of worry
- Never heard from them after Act 1 and hasn't stopped thinking about it
- If she sees them again the relief will be immediate and visible before she can stop it

### Fighting Style

- Two years of deliberate training turned a capable healer into a genuine all-rounder
- Equally comfortable healing, buffing, shielding, and dealing damage — she does not have a weak mode
- Fights as John's backline by design: she keeps him standing, keeps allies functional, and punishes anything that tries to get past him
- Has developed offensive capability specifically so she is never a liability when healing isn't the priority

### Traits

- **Goddess's Faithful.** Luna has advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects from non-divine sources.
- **Empowered Healing.** When Luna restores HP to a creature, they regain an additional 1d8 HP.
- **All-Rounder.** Luna does not suffer disadvantage on attack rolls while she has cast a healing or support spell this turn. She can freely switch between offensive and supportive roles without mechanical penalty.
- **Two as One.** When Luna and John are within 10 ft. of each other, Luna's healing spells restore an additional 1d4 HP when targeting John, and she has advantage on Concentration saves while he is conscious.

- **Spellcasting.** Luna is a 10th level divine spellcaster. Her spellcasting ability is Wisdom (spell save DC 15, +7 to hit). She always has the following spells prepared:
    - _Cantrips:_ Sacred Flame, Guidance, Spare the Dying, Toll the Dead
    - _1st (4 slots):_ Healing Word, Cure Wounds, Bless, Shield of Faith
    - _2nd (3 slots):_ Lesser Restoration, Prayer of Healing, Aid, Spiritual Weapon
    - _3rd (3 slots):_ Mass Healing Word, Revivify, Spirit Guardians
    - _4th (3 slots):_ Guardian of Faith, Death Ward, Banishment
    - _5th (2 slots):_ Mass Cure Wounds, Flame Strike

### Actions

- **Multiattack.** Luna makes two Holy Staff attacks.
- **Holy Staff.** _+4 to hit, reach 5 ft._ Hit: 1d6 bludgeoning + 2d6 radiant.
- **Radiant Burst (Recharge 5–6).** Luna releases a flash of holy light in a 20 ft. radius. Allies in the area regain 3d8+4 HP. Hostile creatures make a DC 15 Con save or are blinded until the end of their next turn.
- **Divine Condemnation.** _One creature within 60 ft._ makes a DC 15 Wis save, taking 4d10 radiant damage on a fail or half on a success. Undead and fiends have disadvantage on this save.

### Bonus Actions

- **Healing Word (4/Day).** Luna calls out to an ally within 60 ft., restoring 1d6+4 HP as a bonus action.
- **Empower Ally.** One ally within 30 ft. gains advantage on their next attack roll or saving throw. Once per short rest.

### Reactions

- **Protective Light.** When an ally within 30 ft. is hit by an attack, Luna can impose disadvantage on the attack roll as she channels a brief flash of divine light to shield them.
- **Intercept.** When John would be reduced to 0 HP while within 30 ft., Luna can expend a spell slot to instead leave him at 1 HP and grant him temporary HP equal to 5 times the slot level used.

### Story Notes (DM Only)

- Luna's worry about the party is genuine and will surface immediately if they reunite — reward players who respond to it
- Michael's death shaped her more than she shows — the right moment will open that up
- Her development into an all-rounder was grief-driven as much as ambition-driven — she never wanted to be in a position where she couldn't do enough again
- Her relationship with John is settled and real — it is also his most visible vulnerability and she knows it
