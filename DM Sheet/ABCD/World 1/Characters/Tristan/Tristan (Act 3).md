_The Demon King, Baal_

- **Role:** Demon King
- **Affiliation:** Demon Kingdom
- **Status:** Alive, Fully Awakened
- **Age:** (Act 3)

### Background

- Born half-demon, raised in [[Arthur]]'s care in the [[Evergreen Forest]] — a quiet life, far from what he would become
- The Church's inquisition, led by Adonis, ended that life
- Awakened as Baal in the chaos that followed — the Demon King's power surfacing through grief and survival
- Has retained memories of both timelines, something no one else in the world can claim
- The players' interference in the past — and their inaction — directly shaped the present: two creatures of the Evergreen Forest were never slain and have since become his Demon Generals
- Convinced the players are agents of a god or gods who have been toying with his life across timelines
- Does not rage about this. He seethes — quietly, coldly, with the particular fury of someone who has decided they are done being a piece on someone else's board

### Personality

- Cold and controlled — the anger is always present and never loud
- Intelligent and deliberate — he does not move without purpose
- Sees the players not as enemies exactly but as instruments of something he resents with his entire being
- Does not monologue. Does not explain himself. Makes his point and moves.

### Relationship with the Players

- Seethes at them with a cold, calm hatred rooted in the belief that they are divine pawns sent to torment him
- Does not underestimate them — their existence across timelines has proven they are worth taking seriously
- Will not kill them carelessly. He wants them to understand what they are part of before anything else

### Relationship with Arthur's Memory

- Complicated in a way he would never admit
- Arthur is the reason he has any restraint at all
- Also the wound that never closed

### Demon Generals

- **The Liger** — formerly a creature of the Evergreen Forest, now fully corrupted and bound to [[Tristan]]'s will
- **The Mistweaver** — same origin, same corruption. Both generals carry the weight of what the players failed to do.

### Will — Stage III (Realization)

**Stage I — Darkness** Raw, instinctual. Coats his body and weapon in shadow. Enhances physical strikes with necrotic weight. Broad and unrefined.

**Stage II — Dominion** Darkness sharpens into active pressure. His Will reaches outward — suppressing, overwhelming, making those around him feel the weight of his presence. Deliberate and controlled.

**Stage III — Oblivion** Dominion stripped of all pretense. He does not suppress — he unmakes. Resistance, conviction, immunity — all of it dissolves on contact. He does not wield it. He is it.

### Fighting Style

- Sword as primary — clean, efficient, no wasted movement
- Dark magic used as pressure and control rather than bombardment
- Will underlies everything — his strikes carry Oblivion at the edges, his magic is saturated with it
- Corruption and influence as a battlefield tool: he does not just fight opponents, he changes them mid-fight

### Special Ability — Corruption

Tristan's nature as the Demon King gives him the ability to corrupt and influence living beings. This is not mind control — it is deeper and slower. He introduces something into a target that begins to rewrite what they are. In short encounters this manifests as a debuff. Over time, in narrative contexts, it is an existential threat.

### Traits

- **Demonic Physiology.** Tristan has advantage on Strength checks and saving throws. He does not need to eat, drink, or sleep.
- **Innate Mana.** Tristan's magic cannot be suppressed by antimagic effects that target external mana sources.
- **Dual Timeline Memory.** Tristan retains memories of both timelines. He cannot be deceived by the players about past events and has advantage on Insight checks against them specifically.
- **Oblivion Aura (Will — Stage III).** Tristan's Will radiates passively. Creatures within 15 ft. of Tristan have disadvantage on saving throws against his abilities. Creatures that fail a saving throw against him also have their damage reduced by 1d6 until the end of their next turn as Oblivion eats at their conviction.
- **Partial Transformation.** When Tristan uses any magic ability, his demonic traits emerge. His AC increases by 1 and he gains 15 temporary HP. If he uses two or more magic abilities in the same encounter, his AC increases by 2 total and he gains an additional 15 temporary HP.
- **Legendary Resistance (2/Day).** If Tristan fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.

### Actions

- **Multiattack.** Tristan makes three Demon Blade attacks. He can replace one with Corrupting Touch or Dark Pulse.
- **Demon Blade.** _+9 to hit, reach 5 ft._ Hit: 1d10+5 slashing + 2d8 necrotic. On a hit the target makes a DC 16 Con save or has their maximum HP reduced by the necrotic damage dealt until they complete a long rest.
- **Corrupting Touch.** _+9 to hit, reach 5 ft._ Hit: 3d8 necrotic damage. The target must make a DC 16 Wisdom save or become Corrupted until the end of their next turn — while Corrupted, they have disadvantage on attacks against Tristan and must make a DC 14 Wisdom save at the start of each turn or use their movement to move toward him.
- **Dark Pulse.** Tristan releases a wave of compressed dark energy in a 20 ft. radius. Each creature makes a DC 16 Con save, taking 4d10 necrotic damage on a fail or half on a success. Triggers Partial Transformation.
- **Oblivion Strike (1/Day, Will).** Tristan channels Stage III Will into a single sword strike. _+9 to hit, reach 5 ft._ Hit: 4d12+5 slashing. The target must make a DC 18 Wisdom save or be Unmade — they lose all resistances and immunities until the end of their next turn as Oblivion strips away what they are.

### Bonus Actions

- **Blink Step.** Tristan teleports up to 40 ft. to an unoccupied space he can see.
- **Corrupt the Wound.** One creature within 30 ft. that is below half HP must make a DC 16 Wisdom save or become Corrupted until the end of their next turn.

### Reactions

- **Instinctive Block.** When hit by an attack, Tristan reduces the damage by 1d10+5.
- **Oblivion Counter.** When a creature within 5 ft. misses Tristan with a melee attack, he can make one Demon Blade attack against them as a reaction.

### Legendary Actions _(2/round, taken at the end of another creature's turn)_

- **Shift (1 action).** Tristan moves up to half his speed without provoking opportunity attacks.
- **Shadow Lash (1 action).** One creature within 30 ft. makes a DC 16 Dex save or takes 2d10 necrotic damage and is knocked prone.
- **Corrupt (2 actions).** One creature within 60 ft. makes a DC 16 Wisdom save or becomes Corrupted for 1 minute. They can repeat the save at the end of each of their turns.
