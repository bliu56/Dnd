**Brother Finn**

- **Role:** Logistics, Camp Setup, Basic Navigation
- **Age:** Late 20s

**Background** A junior Church administrator who volunteered for field duty because the alternative was watching the Church collapse from a desk. Competent, practical, quietly anxious. Originally responsible for supplies, rations, and camp setup. That role has quietly expanded to cover everything nobody else does — which is most things. He sets up camp, breaks it down, cooks when Maren is scouting, mends equipment, tracks everyone's supplies including supplies nobody asked him to track, and has developed a very efficient internal system for doing the work of four people. Nobody in the Hero Party has ever acknowledged this. He has stopped expecting them to.

**Personality** Mild, efficient, easy to overlook. Notices everything. Complains to nobody. Has a very specific and private opinion about each member of the Hero Party that he will never say out loud. Warms up quickly to anyone who says thank you — genuinely, not in passing. That happens rarely enough that it lands when it does.

**Skills:** Supply management, camp setup, ration tracking, road navigation, basic mending and equipment upkeep

**Limitation:** Not a scout. Will not go off road alone. Gets nervous in unfamiliar terrain.

**Relationship with Maren** She comes back from scouting and helps without being asked. Neither of them has ever talked about it. They don't need to.

---

**Sister Maren**

- **Role:** Navigation, Scouting, Basic Cooking
- **Age:** Early 30s

**Background** Spent ten years traveling between Church outposts as a courier before the war made that impossible. Knows roads, terrain, and how to read a landscape. Can move quietly ahead of the group to check what's coming and get back without being seen. Feeds the party when Finn's supplies run short — nothing fancy, but edible and consistent. Is the only reason the Hero Party has not gotten lost twice already and is quietly aware of this. Said something about the workload to Kael exactly once. Received a response that made her decide never to say anything again. Now she just comes back from scouting, sees what Finn is buried under, and helps without making it a conversation.

**Personality** Dry, direct, comfortable with silence. Gets along with nobody in the Hero Party particularly well but respects competence when she sees it. Will give players a straighter answer than anyone else in the group if asked directly about anything. Not bitter exactly — just clear-eyed about what the situation is and who these people are.

**Skills:** Terrain navigation, light scouting, off road travel, basic cooking

**Limitation:** Not a fighter and not a logistician. If Finn goes down the supplies become a problem quickly.

**Relationship with Finn** He does not complain. She does not ask. They have a quiet solidarity that neither of them has named and neither of them needs to.
