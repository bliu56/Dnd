### Background

- Second son of a military noble house that has produced generals and decorated soldiers for three generations
- Did not inherit the title or the estate — that went to his older brother — but inherited the family's absolute certainty that Holt blood produces the finest fighters the world has to offer
- Fought his way into the Hero party through a string of victories against opponents who were all, without exception, weaker than him
- Has never fought anyone stronger
- His entire identity is built on that unbroken foundation and the Holt name behind it

### Personality

- Loud, physical, takes up space deliberately
- The arrogance is bodily and inherited in equal measure — he trusts his body completely and trusts his bloodline's legacy even more
- Has never been given a reason to question either
- Assumes anyone who claims to be stronger is either lying or hasn't met a Holt yet
- Not cruel by nature but completely indifferent to people he has decided don't matter
- Gets along with Kael naturally — two nobles who have decided the world confirms everything they already believed about themselves

### Fighting Style: The Holt Wall

Gareth plants himself and dares things to move him. His shield is not purely defensive — he uses it as a weapon as often as protection, leading with it to create openings and punish anyone who gets too close. His strikes are heavy and deliberate, built on the assumption that he can absorb whatever comes back. He has never needed to be fast. He has never needed to be clever. He hits hard, he doesn't go down, and everything else has always worked itself out. His footwork is minimal because he has never needed to move — opponents come to him and regret it.

### Opinion of the Players

- Sizes them up physically the moment he meets them
- Makes a judgment immediately and will not revise it until something forces him to
- Anyone who doesn't look like a fighter is invisible to him

### Opinion of the Demon King

- Sees the war as an opportunity to prove the Holt name on the largest stage possible
- The Demon King is simply the biggest opponent available
- Does not think about politics or public opinion — that is not a Holt concern

### Story Notes (DM Only)

- Gareth has never been knocked down. The moment it happens — whoever does it — will be the most significant event of his life and he will have no framework for it
- His fighting style has no answer for someone faster or more technically skilled than him — he has never needed one
- [[John (Act 3)|John]] is a hard counter to everything Gareth is built around

### Story Notes (DM Only) — Optional Progression

Gareth can start to notice the party as people rather than obstacles. Entirely optional.

| Stage | Trigger | Response |

| **2 — Pattern recognition** | He starts tracking how they handle things. | "You used that fire spell on the boar earlier. That was smart." Flat. Objective. |

No emotional growth. No vulnerability. Just a guy who's never paid attention to anyone finally having to.
