**Quest I — The Five Named** *fishing*

Somewhere in the world's waters swim five fish that carry names — not given by fishermen, but their own, old and earned. They are not legendary beasts. They are not dangerous. They are simply ancient, and known, and the god who asks this wants them found.

Hunt all five. By rod, by net, by hand — method is irrelevant. What matters is that each one is caught, acknowledged, and released. You will know them when you find them. They will know you too.

_Completion: All five named fish caught and released. Killing them voids the quest._

---

**Quest II — Bested by Better** *battle maniacs*

Find someone stronger than you. Not someone you think you can beat with a clever trick or a lucky roll — someone genuinely, undeniably your better. Fight them. Win.

How you close that gap is your business. Train, prepare, suffer for it. The god asking this is not interested in the victory alone. They are interested in what you become on the way to it.

_Completion: Defeat an opponent of notably higher strength in direct combat. No ambushes. No poison. A real fight._

---

**Quest III — The Single Edge** _Slamming head against brick wall_

You are to enter combat and resolve it — fully, completely — using one attack or one spell only. Not one type. One. A single strike, a single casting, and then nothing more from you until it is over.

Whether that means preparation, patience, or an opening that ends things cleanly is your problem to solve. The god watching this wants to see economy. Restraint. The understanding that power used precisely is worth more than power spent freely.

_Completion: Enter and conclude a genuine combat encounter using a single attack or spell. Allies may act freely. You may not._

---

**Quest IV — No Quarter** _No Mercy_

Someone will cross you. They always do. When it happens — when the line is drawn and stepped over — you are not to let it go. No mercy. No second chances. No looking the other way because it is easier or kinder or less complicated.

The god asking this is not asking you to be cruel. They are asking you to mean what you say. To be someone whose boundaries carry weight. When the moment comes, you will know it. So will they.

_Completion: When genuinely wronged, respond with total and final consequence. The god will know if the moment was real or manufactured._
