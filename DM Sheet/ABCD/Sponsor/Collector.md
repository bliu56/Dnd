**The Collector**

**What he collects:** Lives. Skills, emotions, memories, souls — everything a person is. He doesn't collect objects. He collects *experiences.* Every life is a unique combination he hasn't seen before.

**Why he gives power freely:** He's not generous — he's collecting data. Watching what happens when he gives someone a piece of borrowed ability creates new outcomes, new combinations. It's entertainment. It's research. It's both.

**Why he's interested in the sister:** Her life is unique enough to catch his attention. He wants it. He's patient.

**Connection to Chris/Bowen:** This is the outer god that reaches out when either drops to 0 HP. He offers power — because watching what they do with it is *interesting.*

**The debt:** He claims he's looking for the sister to "repay" her. The truth is simpler — he wants her life. The siblings are his way in.

**Personality:** Chaotic. Friendly. Warm on the surface. The danger is hidden behind charm. He genuinely enjoys these interactions — but his enjoyment doesn't make him safe.
