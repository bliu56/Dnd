# Riddle — The Last Laugh

## Overview

A jester who uncovered a regicide plot. Told the truth as a joke. Executed for it.

---

## Their Life

Riddle was a court jester — not the cleverest, not the funniest, but the one who paid attention. He noticed things others missed: a guard's hand trembling, a servant's eyes flicking to the throne, a pattern in who was assigned to the night watch.

He pieced it together. A regicide plot, weeks in the making, involving half the court. He had no proof, no allies, no power. All he had was his position — the fool who could say anything as long as it was funny.

So he told the truth as a joke. Laid the entire conspiracy bare in a single performance, wrapped in punchlines and pantomime. The court laughed. The conspirators laughed with them, nervous but hidden. No one believed him.

He was arrested the next day for treason — not for knowing the plot, but for being inconvenient. He was executed at dawn. His last words were a punchline no one laughed at.

---

## How the Collector Touched It

The Collector gave him wit and charm — the tools to uncover the plot and to tell it in a way that would be remembered. He watched no one believe him anyway. Collected at the gallows, still smiling, still waiting for someone to get the joke.
