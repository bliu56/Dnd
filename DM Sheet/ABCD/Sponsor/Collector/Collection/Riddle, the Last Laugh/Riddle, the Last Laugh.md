# Riddle — The Last Laugh

## Overview

A jester who uncovered a regicide plot. Told the truth as a joke. Executed for it.

---

## Their Life

Riddle was a court jester — not the cleverest, not the funniest, but the one who paid attention. He noticed things others missed: a guard's hand trembling, a servant's eyes flicking to the throne, a pattern in who was assigned to the night watch.

He pieced it together. A regicide plot, weeks in the making, involving half the court. He had no proof, no allies, no power. All he had was his position — the fool who could say anything as long as it was funny.

So he told the truth as a joke. Laid the entire conspiracy bare in a single performance, wrapped in punchlines and pantomime. The court laughed. The conspirators laughed with them, nervous but hidden. No one believed him.

He was arrested the next day for treason — not for knowing the plot, but for being inconvenient. He was executed at dawn. His last words were a punchline no one laughed at.

---

## How the Collector Touched It

The Collector gave him wit and charm — the tools to uncover the plot and to tell it in a way that would be remembered. He watched no one believe him anyway. Collected at the gallows, still smiling, still waiting for someone to get the joke.

## Power — What Can Be Pulled

The Collector can grant one or more of the following from Riddle's soul.

| Power | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| **The Truth as a Joke** | You cannot be magically compelled to lie. When you tell the truth in a situation where deception would benefit you, you have advantage on the check — and any creature that attempts to discern your intent automatically believes you were joking, being sarcastic, or speaking figuratively. This is a magical effect that bypasses normal Insight checks. |
| **Court Fool's Eye** | You have advantage on all Wisdom (Insight) checks. When a creature lies to you, you instinctively know the truth they are hiding — as a complete, specific fact, not a vague sense. This functions even if the creature is magically protected against truth detection, though you know you are piercing that protection. |
| **Last Laugh** | When you are reduced to 0 HP, you can choose to remain conscious and active for one additional turn. During that turn, your attacks and spells automatically score a critical hit if they hit, and saving throws against your spells or abilities are made with disadvantage. At the end of that turn, you fall unconscious as normal. Once used, this cannot be used again until after a long rest. |
| **The Punchline** | Once per turn, when you deal damage to a creature with an attack or spell, you can add 4d10 psychic damage. The target must then make a Wisdom saving throw (DC = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Charisma modifier). On a failure, it is incapacitated with laughter until the end of your next turn. Creatures that cannot hear you or that lack language are immune to the incapacitation effect but still take the psychic damage. |
