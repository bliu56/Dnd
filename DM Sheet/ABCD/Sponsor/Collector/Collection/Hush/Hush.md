# Hush

## Overview

Born mute. Died at 14. The only one who noticed the war coming. No one listened.

---

## Their Life

Hush was born unable to speak — not from injury, but from a condition no healer could explain. She could hear, could understand, could communicate in gestures and written words, but her voice had never made a sound.

She was the first in her village to notice something was wrong. Animals leaving. Birds silent at dawn. A tension in the air that no one else felt. She tried to warn them — wrote notes, drew pictures, grabbed sleeves and pointed. They patted her head and told her not to worry.

When the war came, her village was the first to fall. Hush was found in the ruins, kneeling in the ash. She had been trying to write a final warning in the dirt when the soldiers found her.

There are records of that war now. Tactics, dates, casualty counts. None of them mention the girl who saw it coming and could not make anyone believe her.

---

## How the Collector Touched It

Natural. Collected after death. A life of perception no one ever valued — the Collector found that combination fascinating. He took her not for what she did, but for what she saw that no one else did.

## Power — What Can Be Pulled

The Collector can grant one or more of the following from Hush's soul.

| Power | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| **Silent Warning** | You can cast *silence* once per long rest without expending a spell slot. The spell originates from a point you can see within 60 ft., not from yourself. |
| **Unheard Truth** | You have advantage on Perception checks. You automatically detect invisible creatures and illusions within 30 ft. that rely on sight to deceive. |
| **The War That Came** | Once per day, you can ask the DM one yes/no question about a threat or danger within the next hour. The DM must answer truthfully. |
| **No Voice, No Echo** | You are immune to effects that require you to be heard. Spells with verbal components cannot be counterspelled when you cast them, as your words carry no sound.
