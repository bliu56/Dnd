# Ivy — of the Endless Garden

## Overview

A woman who grew a garden in a wasteland over 30 years. Destroyed a week after her death.

---

## Their Life

Ivy lived alone at the edge of a wasteland — a stretch of dead earth where nothing had grown for generations. She arrived with nothing but a sack of seeds and a stubbornness that looked like madness from a distance.

She planted. The first year, nothing grew. The second year, a single sprout. She watered it with water she carried from miles away, protected it from wind and scavengers, talked to it like it could hear her. By the fifth year, she had a garden.

Thirty years. She turned a dead stretch of earth into a lush, impossible garden — flowers, vegetables, trees where there had been nothing. Travelers who stumbled on it thought it was a miracle. She told them it was just patience.

She died in her sleep, old and content, surrounded by green. A week later, a passing army — from which side no one could agree — trampled the garden into mud. Within a month, the wasteland had reclaimed it.

---

## How the Collector Touched It

The Collector gave her life-soil magic — the ability to make things grow where they should not. He watched her pour every ounce of it into a place that would not remember her. Collected her at the trampled petals, the garden's last bloom still in her hand.
