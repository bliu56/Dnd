# Ivy — of the Endless Garden

## Overview

A woman who grew a garden in a wasteland over 30 years. Destroyed a week after her death.

---

## Their Life

Ivy lived alone at the edge of a wasteland — a stretch of dead earth where nothing had grown for generations. She arrived with nothing but a sack of seeds and a stubbornness that looked like madness from a distance.

She planted. The first year, nothing grew. The second year, a single sprout. She watered it with water she carried from miles away, protected it from wind and scavengers, talked to it like it could hear her. By the fifth year, she had a garden.

Thirty years. She turned a dead stretch of earth into a lush, impossible garden — flowers, vegetables, trees where there had been nothing. Travelers who stumbled on it thought it was a miracle. She told them it was just patience.

She died in her sleep, old and content, surrounded by green. A week later, a passing army — from which side no one could agree — trampled the garden into mud. Within a month, the wasteland had reclaimed it.

---

## How the Collector Touched It

The Collector gave her life-soil magic — the ability to make things grow where they should not. He watched her pour every ounce of it into a place that would not remember her. Collected her at the trampled petals, the garden's last bloom still in her hand.

## Power — What Can Be Pulled

The Collector can grant one or more of the following from Ivy's soul.

| Power | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| **Life-Soil Magic** | You can cast *goodberry* at will — each berry restores 2 hit points and counts as a full meal. You can cast *plant growth* without components a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest. Once per long rest, you can cast *wall of thorns* or *awaken* without expending a spell slot. |
| **Endless Garden** | When you finish a long rest, you can designate a 30 ft. radius around you as consecrated ground. For the next 24 hours, allies within that area regain an extra 1d10 HP when they spend Hit Dice during a short rest. The area also produces enough edible plants to feed up to 20 people that day. Once per long rest, you can call forth a thicket of thorns and vines in a 30 ft. radius — the area becomes difficult terrain for enemies and provides half cover for you and your allies for 1 minute. |
| **Patience of Ivy** | Once per round — even if it is not your turn — when you fail a saving throw, attack roll, or ability check, you can reroll it. You must use the new roll. This represents Ivy's refusal to accept failure, no matter how many times she was told to stop. |
| **Trampled, Returning** | When you are reduced to 0 HP, you can instead drop to 1 HP. At the start of each of your next three turns, you regain hit points equal to 2d10 + your level — as if the life inside you refuses to stay dead. While this regeneration is active, visible green shoots and petals emerge from your wounds. If you take fire or necrotic damage, the regeneration stops early. Once used, this cannot be used again until after a long rest. |
