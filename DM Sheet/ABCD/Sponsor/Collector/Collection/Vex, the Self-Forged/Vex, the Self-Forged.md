# Vex — the Self-Forged

## Overview

A sorcerer who rebuilt his body into a living weapon. Killed by his own students.

---

## Their Life

Vex was born frail — sickly, weak, the kind of child the world steps over without noticing. He refused to accept it. He taught himself flesh-weaving, a forbidden school of transmutation that allowed him to reshape living tissue. He started with small changes — reinforcing his bones, hardening his skin. He did not stop.

Over decades, Vex turned himself into something unrecognizable. His body became a collection of integrated weapons, reinforced tendons, redundant organs, and self-repairing tissue. He was no longer a man. He was a purpose-built instrument of survival.

He taught his art to students — five of them, each one hand-picked. They studied under him for years. When they had learned everything he could teach, they killed him in his sleep and took his research.

He woke long enough to see them standing over him. He did not curse them. He smiled.

---

## How the Collector Touched It

The Collector gave him the first secret — the initial thread of flesh-weaving magic that started everything. Just a hint, a nudge. He watched Vex spin it into something far beyond what was given. Collected at his peak, when Vex was more weapon than man and still hungry for more.

## Power — What Can Be Pulled

The Collector can grant one or more of the following from Vex's soul.

| Power | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| **Flesh-Weaving** | As a bonus action, you can reshape a part of your body — hardening your skin to gain +2 AC until the start of your next turn, or extending a limb to increase your reach by 5 ft. for one attack. Usable a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest. |
| **Reinforced Frame** | You have resistance to bludgeoning damage. Once per long rest, when you take a critical hit, you can reduce it to a normal hit instead. |
| **Self-Repair** | As a bonus action, you can regain hit points equal to your level. Once per short rest. |
| **Monster's Hunger** | When you reduce a creature to 0 HP, you regain hit points equal to your proficiency bonus + your Constitution modifier.
