# Tess — The Beggar Saint

## Overview

A street urchin turned cultist, betrayed, then a wanderer doing quiet kindnesses. Died in a gutter.

---

## Their Life

Tess was born on the street — no family, no name, no record of ever existing. She survived by staying small, staying quiet, and staying unnoticed. When a cult found her, they offered her something she had never had: belonging. She joined them completely, gave them everything.

They betrayed her. Sacrificed her position to save themselves during a raid. She was left to take the fall alone.

She survived. She walked away from the cult, from the streets, from everyone. She became a wanderer — no destination, no purpose except a quiet rule she had made for herself: if she saw someone in need, she helped. No sermons. No preaching. Just a hand where one was needed.

She did this for years. No one knew her name. No one asked. She died in a gutter, alone, after giving her last food to a child she did not know.

---

## How the Collector Touched It

Natural. The Collector never touched her life. He simply watched it unfold — a life of quiet tragedy so rare and pure that it was worth collecting without intervention. He found her after death and added her to the collection, untouched, unchanged.

## Power — What Can Be Pulled

The Collector can grant one or more of the following from Tess's soul.

| Power | Effect |
|-------|--------|
| **Unnoticed Passage** | You can cast *pass without trace* and *invisibility* at will — no spell slot or component required. Only one can be active at a time. While either is active, you cannot be targeted by divination magic or scrying sensors of any level. |
| **Quiet Kindness** | As a bonus action, touch a creature. It regains hit points equal to 6d8 + your level. Usable once per short rest. |
| **Street Survivor** | You are always considered hidden for the purpose of Sneak Attack and similar features, as long as you have any form of concealment — including light obscurement, crowds, or dim light. You cannot be tracked by nonmagical means, and you have advantage on all Dexterity (Stealth) checks. |
| **No Name, No Record** | You are immune to effects that would read your thoughts, surface your memories, or discern your true name. You have advantage on saving throws against any spell or effect that would reveal your location, identity, or intentions. Divination spells of 5th level or lower cannot detect you unless you allow it. |
