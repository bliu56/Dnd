
## Overview

A holy knight who broke his vows to save a child. Killed his own order. Died holding the child.

---

## Their Life

Ser Valdris was a paladin of the Church — devoted, unquestioning, the kind of man other knights looked up to. That changed when his order was ordered to execute a child accused of demon influence. The child was innocent. Valdris knew it. When his brothers drew their swords, he drew his too — against them.

He killed every knight in that room. Not in a blind rage — in a calculated, methodical slaughter. Each one was a man he had trained beside, shared meals with, trusted. He killed them anyway.

He carried the child out of the temple and vanished. He spent the next several years as a fugitive, protecting the child and others like them — anyone the Church deemed expendable. He never took another life for the Church again. He only ever used his sword to protect.

He died holding that same child — now grown — during a raid. The Collector was watching.

---

## How the Collector Touched It

The Collector gave him the strength to fight back. Not much — just enough to turn the tide in that room. He wanted to see what a man like Valdris would do when pushed past his breaking point. The answer was *everything.*

The Collector collected him at the moment of his death — surrounded by the bodies of the enemies who had finally brought him down, the child he saved crouched beside him, alive.
