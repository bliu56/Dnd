**Trigger Event — Chris or Bowen Drops to 0 HP**

The world stops.

The bird frozen mid-wing above your head — like it was never real. The trees caught mid-sway, refusing to move. The blade in your enemy's hand, stopped an inch from your skin. Your own heartbeat — silent.

Then the color drains. Flesh to grey. Sky to white. Blood to nothing. The world becomes a charcoal sketch.

Then the lines start to fall. The edge of a face drops. The outline of a building slides down. One by one, the world collapses into a single straight line stretching across nothing. And then that line opens beneath you.

You fall.

You land in a sitting room.

A study — warm amber light, a crackling fireplace, bookshelves lining every wall. A plush armchair facing you. A low table with two cups of tea, still steaming, like you were expected.

It's cozy. It's comfortable. It's *wrong.*

The fire has no heat. The books have no titles. The cups — you look away for a second — have moved closer to you when you look back.

A figure is already seated in the armchair across from you. Cross-legged. Leaning back like this is their favorite spot in the world.

**The Collector:** "Ah. There you are. I've been looking for you."

It smiles. Friendly. Warm. Wrong.

**The Collector:** "I'm an ally. You don't know me yet. But I know you. And right now, you need help."

It leans forward.

**The Collector:** "So here's the deal. I give you what you need — you get back up, you win this fight. In return, when the time comes, I ask for a favor. Simple."

It extends a hand.

**The Collector:** "Do we have an agreement?"

---

**If the player asks who they are or why they're helping:**

The Collector's smile doesn't waver, but it holds a beat longer than natural.

**The Collector:** "Let's say I have history with your family. Good history. There's someone I need to find — someone connected to you. Help me find them, and we're even."

It doesn't elaborate. The warmth stays, but the door is closed.

---

**If they accept:** They snap back to consciousness — standing, healed enough to fight. The Collector's power surges through them. Cost to be determined later.

**If they refuse:** The darkness lingers. The Collector tilts its head.

**The Collector:** "Think about it. I'll be here when you change your mind."

It fades. They wake on the ground. The world rushes back — color, sound, motion.
