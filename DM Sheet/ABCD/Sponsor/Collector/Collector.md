**The Collector**

**Role:** The entity that holds the older sister's debt. Claims to have been her sponsor/friend.

**Connection to Chris/Bowen:** This is the outer god that reaches out when either drops to 0 HP. It offers power — at a price.

**The debt:** The older sister owes something. She's unreachable right now, so the Collector plans to use Chris and Bowen to get to her.

**Personality:** Chaotic. Gives power freely but always collects. Friendly on the surface — the danger is hidden behind charm.
