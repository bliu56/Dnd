
**Session 1 Summary**

---
**The Gate — Emmet's Request**

The party met Emmet Ashford at [[Stonewood]]'s south gate. He told them his story — the Duskbloom, [[Sera]], his father's flowers, the asking. They heard all of it. They left him behind anyway. The debate was real: Connor and Pranav wanted to bring him, Bowen and Chris didn't, Andrew stayed neutral, Darrin thought he was too old for the errand. The opposition held. Emmet stayed in Stonewood.

---
**The Twilight Forest**

They made it through. The Ashcrawler — the bear — they ghosted entirely, the whole party slipping past without engaging. The Veilstag they killed. Pranav landed a particularly good insult somewhere in that fight. They came out the other side intact.

---
**Lowfield — Days 1 and 2**

They didn't stay at the inn. They camped the outskirts and ran their own investigation, asking villagers about a structure in the forest. Day 1 gave them nothing useful. Nobody talked.

Day 2 they found a battlefield near the house — the site of a fight between [[Arthur]] and the Church. They found it on their own, without being pointed there.

Between Day 2 and Day 3, Darrin started seeing things at night. One eye, then two, a white deer, a white butterfly, then three eyes, then four. He dragged the party out to investigate. Nothing came of it — nothing they could find or touch. The party probably filed it under strange forest and moved on.

---
**Day 3 — Vael'shar and the House**

On the way to the house, Vael'shar found them first. It attacked Pranav in secret, then used Hollow Touch on Bowen — reaching in and pulling out what he was most afraid of losing: his missing older sister. It shifted into her form, injured, emerging from the trees, pointing behind the party. It didn't land. The party didn't bite. They pushed on to the house.

---
**The House — The Demon King**

At Arthur's house they met a man they didn't recognize. He knew them. He clocked immediately that they didn't know who he was and said nothing to correct it. The exchange was calm. He promised them safe passage back to Lowfield and advised them not to leave with Michael, Luna, and John.

They took the promise. They went back.

Darrin named his hawk Maki at some point during all of this.
