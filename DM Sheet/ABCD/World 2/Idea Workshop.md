# World 2 — Player Vote

> *One of these three settings will be the second world for the campaign. Read each description and vote for the one that sounds most interesting.*

---

## 1) Mystery

A single city and its outskirts. There's something here that needs to be solved — a disappearance, a death, a secret buried in plain sight. Figure out what's really going on before it's too late.

**Vibe:** Investigation, social politics, confined setting. Fighting is present but not the main focus.

---

## 2) Hunt — The Competition

A kingdom-wide beast hunt. Rival parties compete to track and slay mythical creatures — ancient dragons, unique monsters — for glory, prizes, and the king's favor. Secure sponsorships from merchants, earn noble backing, and outmaneuver your competition to claim the crown.

**Vibe:** High-octane monster hunting, rivalries, resource management. Open world within a region. Think *Monster Hunter* meets a competitive tournament arc.

---

## 3) War — Asymmetric

A war — but not a fair one. Your side is outmatched, outnumbered, or outmaneuvered by an enemy commander of terrifying intelligence. This is not a battle of armies. It is a battle of decisions, sacrifices, and finding the one crack in an otherwise unbeatable strategy.

**Vibe:** Guerrilla tactics, siege mentality, desperate gambits. Darker tone. Think *Code Geass* or *Attack on Titan* from the losing side.
